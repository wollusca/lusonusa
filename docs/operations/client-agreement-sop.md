# SOP: Quotes, client confirmation and the 14-day withdrawal right

For every LUSONUSA professional. **Status: draft based on research, to be reviewed by a lawyer when one is available.** Legal basis: Decreto-Lei 24/2014 (arts. 4, 6, 9, 10, 15, 17); see `docs/research/portugal-consumer-website-law.md`.

## Why this exists
Private clients (consumers) can cancel a service contract within **14 days** without giving a reason. That does **not** mean we have to wait 14 days, and it is **not** a "refund if unhappy" right:
- If the client **asks in writing** for us to start now and **acknowledges** the consequences, we can start immediately.
- If they cancel mid-way, **they pay for the work already done** (art. 15).
- If the service is **fully completed** at their request, the right to cancel **ends** (art. 17(1)(a)).

If we skip the written request, the client could cancel for up to 12 months and pay **nothing** for work done. Following these steps every time prevents that.

**Business clients** (companies, or professionals buying for their business) do not have this right. Use the B2B variant in step 2.

## Step by step

| # | Step | Who | Record to keep |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Enquiry and free first orientation. No contract yet | Aditya, then the professional | — |
| 2 | Send the **written quote** (template A), in Portuguese plus the client's language | Professional delivering the service | Quote message or PDF |
| 3 | Client replies with the **acceptance + early-start text** (template B), word for word | Client | Their reply (screenshot or email) |
| 4 | Send a short **confirmation** (template C) before starting | Professional | Confirmation message |
| 5 | Take the 50% down payment, then start the work | Professional | Payment proof |
| 6 | If the client cancels: calculate the pro-rata amount (see below) and refund the rest within 14 days | Professional | Calculation and refund proof |
| 7 | On completion: send the completion message (template D) and the balance invoice (due in 14 days) | Professional | Message and fatura-recibo |
| 8 | Save the **evidence pack** (quote, acceptance, confirmation, payments, completion) for **5 years** | Professional | Folder per client |

### Golden rules
1. **Silence or a 👍 is not consent.** The client must send the full text (template B), or reply "Concordo / I agree / Saya setuju" **directly to** the template message. Ask again if not.
2. **The quote names one professional**, with full name, NIF and contact. That professional invoices and gets paid into their own account. Never a shared account.
3. **Portuguese is always included** for consumers in Portugal; it's legally required. Add English or Indonesian alongside.
4. **Email or a PDF is the safest form.** WhatsApp is acceptable when you keep the chat. Don't delete it.
5. **Never start paid work before step 3.** Free orientation (step 1) is fine anytime.
6. **Extra scope or time:** send a new mini-quote (template E) and get "I agree" before doing it.

## Pro-rata calculation (if a consumer cancels after starting)
Amount due = agreed total price × share of the service actually performed before they told us, plus documented out-of-pocket costs (e.g. notary fees already paid).
Example: package €300 covering 4 accompaniments; 1 done → €75 due (+ €20 notary fee already paid) = €95. The down payment was €150, so refund €55 within 14 days.
No flat penalty or cancellation fee.

## Templates

### A. Quote (consumer)
**PT**
> Olá [nome], segue o orçamento para o serviço que pediu:
> **Serviço:** [descrição clara, ex.: acompanhamento à AIMA no dia 12/11, incl. preparação de documentos]
> **Prestado por:** [nome completo], NIF [nº], [morada], [email/telefone], em nome próprio (trabalhador independente).
> **Preço total:** €[valor] ([IVA incluído / isento de IVA ao abrigo do art. 53.º do CIVA]). Taxas oficiais (ex.: notário, AIMA) não incluídas, pagas diretamente.
> **Pagamento:** 50% para iniciar; restante até 14 dias após a conclusão.
> **Prazo previsto:** [datas]
> **Livre resolução:** tem 14 dias para resolver o contrato sem custos e sem indicar motivo. Informação completa e formulário: lusonusa.agency/termos.html
> **Reclamações:** Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico (livroreclamacoes.pt) · Resolução alternativa de litígios: CACCL / CNIACC.
> Para aceitar e pedir que comecemos já, responda com o texto abaixo.

**EN** (send together with the PT)
> Hi [name], here is the quote for the service you asked for:
> **Service:** […] **Provided by:** [full name], NIF […], [address], [contact], acting in their own name (self-employed).
> **Total price:** €[…] ([VAT included / VAT-exempt under art. 53 CIVA]). Official fees (e.g. notary, AIMA) not included, paid directly.
> **Payment:** 50% to start; balance within 14 days of completion. **Timing:** […]
> **Right of withdrawal:** you have 14 days to cancel without cost or reason. Full information and model form: lusonusa.agency/terms.html
> **Complaints:** Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico · ADR: CACCL / CNIACC.
> To accept and ask us to start now, reply with the text below.

**ID** (send together with the PT)
> Halo [nama], berikut penawaran untuk layanan yang Anda minta:
> **Layanan:** […] **Dikerjakan oleh:** [nama lengkap], NIF […], [alamat], [kontak], atas nama pribadi (pekerja mandiri).
> **Total biaya:** €[…] ([termasuk IVA / bebas IVA berdasarkan pasal 53 CIVA]). Biaya resmi (mis. notaris, AIMA) tidak termasuk dan dibayar langsung.
> **Pembayaran:** 50% di awal; sisanya paling lambat 14 hari setelah selesai. **Waktu:** […]
> **Hak pembatalan:** Anda punya 14 hari untuk membatalkan tanpa biaya dan tanpa alasan. Info lengkap dan formulir: lusonusa.agency/terms.html
> **Pengaduan:** Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico · Penyelesaian sengketa: CACCL / CNIACC.
> Untuk menyetujui dan meminta kami mulai sekarang, balas dengan teks di bawah ini.

**B2B variant:** replace the withdrawal line with: "As a business client, the consumer right of withdrawal does not apply. Cancellation follows our Terms, section 9." No template B needed; a plain "I accept" is enough.

### B. Client's acceptance + early start (the client sends this back)
**PT**
> Aceito o orçamento de [data] para [serviço]. Peço expressamente que o serviço comece antes do fim do prazo de 14 dias de livre resolução. Tomo conhecimento de que, se resolver o contrato, pagarei o valor proporcional ao serviço já prestado, e de que perco o direito de livre resolução quando o serviço estiver totalmente prestado.

**EN**
> I accept the quote of [date] for [service]. I expressly ask for the service to start before the 14-day withdrawal period ends. I understand that if I cancel, I will pay for the part of the service already provided, and that I lose the right to withdraw once the service has been fully provided.

**ID**
> Saya menyetujui penawaran tanggal [tanggal] untuk [layanan]. Saya secara tegas meminta agar layanan dimulai sebelum masa pembatalan 14 hari berakhir. Saya memahami bahwa jika saya membatalkan, saya membayar bagian layanan yang sudah dikerjakan, dan bahwa hak pembatalan saya berakhir setelah layanan selesai sepenuhnya.

Practical tip: send template B as a **separate message right after the quote**, so the client can copy, paste and send it.

### C. Confirmation before starting
> PT: Obrigado, [nome]. Confirmamos o serviço [descrição] por €[valor], com início a [data], a seu pedido expresso. Guarde esta conversa como confirmação do contrato.
> EN: Thank you, [name]. We confirm the service [description] for €[…], starting on [date] at your express request. Please keep this conversation as confirmation of the contract.
> ID: Terima kasih, [nama]. Kami konfirmasi layanan [deskripsi] seharga €[…], mulai [tanggal] atas permintaan tegas Anda. Simpan percakapan ini sebagai konfirmasi kontrak.

### D. Completion
> PT: O serviço [descrição] ficou concluído hoje, [data]. Segue a fatura do valor restante (€[…]), a pagar até [data + 14 dias]. Obrigado pela confiança!
> EN: The service [description] was completed today, [date]. Attached is the invoice for the balance (€[…]), due by [date + 14 days]. Thank you for your trust!
> ID: Layanan [deskripsi] telah selesai hari ini, [tanggal]. Terlampir tagihan sisa pembayaran (€[…]), jatuh tempo [tanggal + 14 hari]. Terima kasih atas kepercayaan Anda!

### E. Extra scope or overtime (ask before doing it)
> PT: A [atividade] está a demorar mais do que o previsto ([x] h até agora). Continuar custa €[valor]/h (estimativa: [y] h). Quer que continuemos? Responda "Concordo" para avançar.
> EN: [Activity] is taking longer than planned ([x] h so far). Continuing costs €[…]/h (estimate: [y] h). Shall we continue? Reply "I agree" to go ahead.
> ID: [Kegiatan] memakan waktu lebih lama dari rencana (sudah [x] jam). Melanjutkan dikenakan €[…]/jam (perkiraan: [y] jam). Lanjutkan? Balas "Saya setuju" untuk melanjutkan.
