# Editorial Calendar: Nov 2026 – Apr 2027

**Cadence:** 4 articles/month. Each month has **2 NUSA** pieces (written in Bahasa Indonesia first, EN second) and **2 LUSO** pieces (written in European Portuguese first, EN second). A third language is added only where the keyword map shows demand (column "Langs").
**Authors are an assignable field.** The "Author" column below shows the **default** author from the current team (contract team table). Any piece may be reassigned to a new team member or a guest expert (e.g. a partner lawyer for a regulatory piece). Mark it `New/guest author: TBD` until they're onboarded via the checklist in §4. Every article shows "By <author> · Last reviewed <date> by <reviewer>", and links to the author's team or author page (`Person` schema). Regulatory pieces also have a named **Reviewed by / Updated by** owner (see "Reviewed by / updated by" at the end of §1).
**Primary keyword** comes from `keyword-map.md`. **Primary service** is the page the article's main CTA points to (`site-architecture.md` §6).
**Slug** = the EN slug under `/guides/`. The PT slug goes under `/pt/guias/` and the ID slug under `/id/panduan/`. Localised slugs are listed only where they differ meaningfully.

## 4. Adding a new author (team member or guest expert)

Complete this before their first byline goes live:

- [ ] **Identity & consent:** full name as it should appear (no pseudonyms), written consent to publish their name, photo and bio. Guest experts also confirm their firm allows the byline.
- [ ] **Role label & title:** in EN, PT and ID. For guests, give the real professional title (e.g. "Advogada, [firm]") and add "Guest author". Never give anyone a title they don't hold (no "sworn translator", no "lawyer" unless they are a licensed one).
- [ ] **Bio:** 2–3 lines per language, with verifiable credentials (degree, registration number for regulated professions such as the Ordem dos Advogados number *[verify the display rules]*), years of experience and languages.
- [ ] **Photo:** square, ≥ 600 px, WebP/JPEG ≤ 80 KB, under the agreed asset folder (Stream 1/2 owns asset paths. In Phase 2 use `src/content/team/` or the equivalent).
- [ ] **LinkedIn / professional profile URL:** used for `sameAs`.
- [ ] **Author registry entry:** add them to `articles/authors.js` (being created by Stream 2) in Phase 1, and to `src/content/team/<slug>.json` in Phase 2. Fields: `id` (slug), `name`, `role`, `title` (per language), `bio` (per language), `photo`, `sameAs`, `isGuest`, `knowsLanguage`.
- [ ] **Author page:** `/about/<slug>/` (team) or `/guides/authors/<slug>/` (guest) *[Phase 2 decision]*, with `Person` schema. Guests have `worksFor` set to their own organisation, not LUSONUSA.
