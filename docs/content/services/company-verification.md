### H1
Indonesian company verification and supplier due diligence

### Value proposition
Before you sign a contract or send a deposit, make sure the Indonesian company is who it says it is. ### Who it's for
- Portuguese importers and buyers about to place a first order
- Companies asked to pay a deposit to a new Indonesian supplier

- Firms that received an offer that looks "too good" or changed bank details

### What's included
**Desk verification**
- Legal-entity check on AHU Online (registration, deed data, directors, commissioners, shareholders where available)
- NIB and business licence check on OSS, including whether the KBLI matches the products offered
- NPWP (tax ID) validation where possible
- Sector-specific checks (e.g. OJK for financial firms, BPOM for food/cosmetics)
- Sanctions and adverse-media screening

- Video call with the counterpart to confirm their role and authority against the registry data (we don't collect copies of their personal ID)

**Extended verification (optional)**
- Site visit to the factory, warehouse or office, with photos and notes
- Reference checks with customers or partners

**Not included:** legal opinions, credit ratings or financial audits, litigation searches requiring a licensed lawyer (available via licensed partner [PARTNER: owner to name]), investment advice, guarantees about future performance.

### What we don't do / when you need a lawyer
- We don't issue legal opinions on a company, its contracts or its licences — a licensed Indonesian law firm does, and we can refer you [PARTNER: owner to name].

### FAQs
**How can I check if an Indonesian company is real?**
Start with the AHU Online registry of the Ministry of Law and the company's NIB on OSS. A real PT has a deed, a Ministry approval and an NIB tied to specific KBLI codes. We check these and compare them with what the company tells you.

**What are common warning signs?**
Bank account in a personal name or a different company, pressure to pay quickly, free email addresses, prices far below market, no physical address, and documents that don't match the registry.

**Can you check a supplier's export licence?**
We check the NIB and licences on OSS and whether the KBLI codes cover the products and export activity. Product-specific permits (e.g. BPOM, halal certificate, SVLK for timber [verify]) are checked where relevant.

**Do you give a legal opinion on the company?**
No. We report facts from official sources and our own checks. If you need a legal opinion, we coordinate with a licensed Indonesian law firm.

**Will the company know it is being checked?**
Desk checks are discreet. Video calls and site visits are done openly and professionally.

**How recent is the information?**
Registries reflect what has been filed; recent changes may not yet appear. The report shows the date of every check.

**Can I send you my documents on WhatsApp?**
Business documents — the supplier's offer, invoices, company certificates or registry extracts — yes, by WhatsApp or email. Personal ID documents (passports, ID cards) no: for security we don't receive ID documents digitally, and we don't need them for a company check.

**Até que ponto a informação está atualizada?**
