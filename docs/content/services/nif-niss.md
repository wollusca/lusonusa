### FAQs
**Can I get a NIF before I arrive in Portugal?**
Yes. A non-resident can be assigned a NIF through a representative with a power of attorney, who applies on your behalf (e.g. via e-Balcão on Portal das Finanças). We coordinate the paperwork; the representative must be someone you appoint.

**Do I need a tax representative to get a NIF?**
According to the Tax Authority, you don't need a tax representative just to be given a NIF. However, if you live outside the EU/EEA and have a tax relationship in Portugal (a job, a rental, property or self-employment), you must either appoint a tax representative or sign up for electronic notifications on Portal das Finanças [verify].

**How much does a NIF cost?**
The NIF is free when you request it from the Tax Authority. Companies that charge for "a NIF" are charging for their service; ours is listed above.

**What is the NISS and when do I need it?**
The NISS is your Portuguese social security number. You need it to work legally (your employer declares you with it), to access certain benefits and, in some cases, for your residence permit file. You can request it online on seg-social.pt or your employer can request it.

**What documents do I need for the NIF?**
Usually your valid passport and, depending on your situation, a residence document or visa and proof of address. If someone applies for you, they also need a power of attorney. Exact requirements vary by office — we send you a checklist after triage.

**I already have a NIF with an Indonesian address. What now?**
Once you live in Portugal, update your address with the Tax Authority. If you keep a non-EU address while having a tax relationship in Portugal, the tax representative / electronic notifications rule applies [verify].

**Can I send you my documents on WhatsApp?**
No — for your security we don't receive ID or immigration documents digitally. Before the appointment we talk you through your situation and send you a checklist; we check your documents in person and you keep them. Where the official portal asks for a scan (e.g. the NISS form on seg-social.pt), you upload it yourself and we guide you through each step.

### Perguntas frequentes
**É possível obter o NIF antes de chegar a Portugal?**
Sim. Um não residente pode obter NIF através de um representante com procuração, que faz o pedido em seu nome (por exemplo, via e-Balcão no Portal das Finanças).

**É obrigatório ter representante fiscal para pedir o NIF?**
Segundo a AT, não é necessário nomear representante fiscal apenas para a atribuição do NIF. Contudo, um residente fora da UE/EEE com relação jurídico-tributária em Portugal (trabalho, arrendamento, imóvel, atividade) deve nomear representante fiscal ou aderir às notificações eletrónicas [verify].

**Quanto custa o NIF?**
O NIF é gratuito quando pedido à AT. Quem cobra "um NIF" está a cobrar o serviço de apoio.

**O que é o NISS e quando é necessário?**
É o Número de Identificação da Segurança Social. É necessário para trabalhar (a entidade empregadora comunica o trabalhador com este número) e para aceder a prestações. Pode ser pedido online em seg-social.pt ou pela entidade empregadora.

**Que documentos são necessários?**
Normalmente passaporte válido e, consoante o caso, visto ou título de residência e comprovativo de morada; se outra pessoa fizer o pedido, procuração. Os requisitos podem variar — enviamos a lista após a triagem.

### Tanya jawab
**Bisa bikin NIF sebelum berangkat ke Portugal?**
Bisa. WNI yang belum tinggal di Portugal bisa mendapat NIF lewat perwakilan yang memegang surat kuasa (procuração), yang mengajukan atas nama Anda, misalnya lewat e-Balcão di Portal das Finanças. Kami bantu koordinasi dokumennya.

**Apakah wajib punya representante fiscal untuk bikin NIF?**
Menurut otoritas pajak Portugal, tidak wajib hanya untuk mendapatkan NIF. Tapi kalau alamat Anda di luar Uni Eropa/EEA dan Anda punya urusan pajak di Portugal (kerja, sewa rumah, punya properti, atau usaha), Anda wajib menunjuk representante fiscal **atau** mengaktifkan notifikasi elektronik di Portal das Finanças [verify].

**Bikin NIF bayar berapa?**
NIF gratis kalau diurus langsung ke Autoridade Tributária. Kalau ada yang "menjual NIF", yang dibayar sebenarnya jasa pendampingannya.

**NISS itu apa dan kapan perlu?**
NISS adalah nomor jaminan sosial Portugal (mirip BPJS Ketenagakerjaan). Anda perlu NISS untuk bekerja secara legal — kantor Anda melaporkan Anda dengan nomor ini — dan kadang juga untuk berkas izin tinggal. NISS bisa diajukan online di seg-social.pt atau oleh pemberi kerja.

**Dokumen apa saja yang dibutuhkan?**
Biasanya paspor yang masih berlaku dan, tergantung kondisi, visa atau kartu izin tinggal serta bukti alamat. Kalau diurus orang lain, perlu surat kuasa. Syarat tiap kantor bisa sedikit beda — checklist lengkap kami kirim setelah konsultasi awal.

**NIF saya masih alamat Indonesia. Harus bagaimana?**
Setelah Anda tinggal di Portugal, segera ganti alamat di Finanças. Selama alamatnya masih di luar Uni Eropa dan Anda punya urusan pajak di Portugal, aturan representante fiscal / notifikasi elektronik tetap berlaku [verify].

**Sudah lapor diri ke KBRI Lisabon?**
Ini bukan syarat NIF, tapi kami sarankan semua WNI di Portugal lapor diri ke KBRI Lisabon (bisa online lewat portal Peduli WNI) supaya mudah dibantu kalau ada keadaan darurat [verify].

**Boleh saya kirim dokumen lewat WhatsApp?**
Tidak — demi keamanan Anda, kami tidak menerima dokumen identitas atau imigrasi secara digital. Sebelum janji temu kita ngobrol dulu soal kondisi Anda dan kami kirimkan checklist; dokumen dicek langsung saat bertemu dan tetap Anda pegang. Kalau portal resmi meminta scan (misalnya formulir NISS di seg-social.pt), Anda sendiri yang mengunggahnya di portal tersebut, dan kami pandu langkah demi langkah.
