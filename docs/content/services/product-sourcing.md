### SEO
- **Title (54):** Product Sourcing in Indonesia for Portuguese Importers

### H1
Product sourcing and supplier management in Indonesia

### Value proposition
Indonesia makes excellent furniture, handicrafts, textiles, coffee, spices and other goods, but finding the right factory from Europe is slow and risky. We shortlist suppliers that fit your product, verify them, handle samples and day-to-day communication in Bahasa Indonesia, and keep you informed on Lisbon time, from first enquiry to shipment.

### Who it's for
- Portuguese importers, retailers and interior/hospitality buyers
- Brands looking for a manufacturing partner (private label or OEM)
- Distributors of commodities and food ingredients (coffee, cocoa, spices, coconut products)
- Companies whose current Indonesian supplier is unreliable or unresponsive

### What's included
- Product brief: specifications, target price, volumes, certifications, delivery terms (Incoterms)
- Market scan and longlist of manufacturers/exporters; shortlist of 3–5 with comparison table
- Basic verification of shortlisted suppliers (AHU, NIB/OSS, export capacity) — full due diligence available via [company verification](/services/indonesian-company-verification/)
- Requests for quotation, sample coordination and consolidated sample shipping
- Communication with factories in Bahasa Indonesia; meeting facilitation (video or in person)
- Order follow-up: production milestones, photos, pre-shipment checklist
- **Sourcing Retainer**: ongoing supplier management for repeat orders, monthly reporting

**Not included:** buying or reselling goods on our own account (we do not take title or margin on goods unless agreed in writing [verify commercial model]); contract drafting (done by your lawyer or a licensed partner); third-party lab testing or certified inspection (coordinated with an inspection partner [PARTNER: owner to name], fees separate); freight, insurance and duties.

### Process & typical timeline
1. **Brief & scoping call** — 1 week.
2. **Longlist and shortlist** — 2–4 weeks, depending on product complexity.
3. **Verification of shortlisted suppliers** — 1 week (in parallel).
4. **Samples** — 2–6 weeks including production and courier *(supplier and courier dependent)*.
5. **Negotiation and first order** — 1–3 weeks.
6. **Production follow-up and pre-shipment check** — per order lead time (often 4–12 weeks for furniture) *(supplier dependent)*.
7. **Ongoing (retainer)** — monthly updates, reorders, issue handling.

### Price
**Sourcing project from €[PRICE: owner to set]**; **Sourcing Retainer from €[PRICE: owner to set]/month** — see `/pricing/`. Supplier verification is priced per company (**Supplier Verification** package). Start with a **free 15-minute triage call**; a paid case assessment is credited if you proceed.
Samples, courier costs, inspections, lab tests, travel, freight and duties are separate and paid by the client.

### FAQs
**How do I find a reliable supplier in Indonesia?**
Define the product precisely, shortlist several manufacturers, verify their registration and export experience, test samples and visit if volumes justify it. Trade fairs such as Trade Expo Indonesia or IFEX (furniture) [verify] are also useful.

**What is the minimum order quantity?**
It depends on the product. Handicraft workshops can accept small runs; furniture factories often expect a mixed container. We tell you early if your volume is realistic.

**Do you charge suppliers a commission?**
Our model is a client-paid fee so our advice stays on your side. [verify commercial model with owner]

**Which products need extra attention in the EU?**
Wood, palm oil, cocoa, coffee, rubber, soy and cattle products fall under the EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) [verify application date], and food and cosmetics need EU compliance. Our [export-import advisory](/services/export-import-portugal-indonesia/) covers this.

**Can you inspect goods before shipment?**
We coordinate pre-shipment checks and photos; certified third-party inspections or lab tests are arranged with an inspection partner at extra cost.

**How do I pay a new supplier safely?**
Pay only to a company bank account in the supplier's legal name, after verification, and agree staged payments. Joko Supriyanto can explain common payment options (e.g. letters of credit); we don't sell financial products.

### Perguntas frequentes
**Como encontro um fornecedor fiável na Indonésia?**
Defina o produto com precisão, selecione vários fabricantes, verifique o registo e a experiência de exportação, teste amostras e visite as instalações se o volume o justificar. Feiras como a Trade Expo Indonesia ou a IFEX (mobiliário) [verify] também ajudam.

**Qual é a quantidade mínima de encomenda?**
Depende do produto. Oficinas de artesanato aceitam séries pequenas; fábricas de mobiliário esperam muitas vezes um contentor misto. Dizemos-lhe cedo se o seu volume é realista.

**Cobram comissão aos fornecedores?**
O nosso modelo baseia-se em honorários pagos pelo cliente, para que o nosso acompanhamento esteja do seu lado. [verify commercial model with owner]

**Que produtos exigem cuidado adicional na UE?**
Madeira, óleo de palma, cacau, café, borracha, soja e produtos bovinos estão abrangidos pelo Regulamento Europeu sobre Desflorestação (EUDR) [verify application date]; alimentos e cosméticos têm de cumprir as regras da UE. O nosso [apoio à exportação e importação](/pt/servicos/exportar-para-indonesia/) trata destes temas.

**Podem inspecionar a mercadoria antes do embarque?**
Coordenamos verificações e fotografias antes do embarque; inspeções certificadas ou ensaios laboratoriais são feitos por um parceiro de inspeção, com custo adicional.

**Como pago a um fornecedor novo com segurança?**
Pague apenas para uma conta bancária em nome da empresa fornecedora, depois da verificação, e acorde pagamentos faseados. Joko Supriyanto pode explicar as modalidades de pagamento habituais (por ex. crédito documentário); não vendemos produtos financeiros.

Pencarian dan pengelolaan supplier Indonesia untuk pembeli dari Portugal
