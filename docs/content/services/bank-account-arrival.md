### FAQs
**Can a foreigner open a bank account in Portugal?**
Yes. Banks usually ask for a valid passport, a Portuguese NIF, proof of address and proof of income, employment or student enrolment. Some banks accept non-residents. Each bank sets its own requirements.

**Do I need a NIF to open a bank account?**
In practice, yes — Portuguese banks require a NIF. Get it first; we can do both together.

**What is a basic bank account (serviços mínimos bancários)?**
A Portuguese regime that gives access to a basic account with a limited, regulated annual cost [verify current cap]. It may suit people who only need essential services. Check conditions on the Banco de Portugal customer portal.

**Can I open an account before arriving in Portugal?**
Some banks offer remote opening for non-residents, but requirements are stricter and not every bank does it. We'll tell you what is realistic for your case — without recommending a specific bank.

**Why do I need a Portuguese phone number?**
Banks use it for security codes and app verification, and MB WAY works with a Portuguese mobile number. That's why we set up the SIM card first.

**Which bank do you recommend?**
We don't recommend or promote any bank, and we don't sell financial products. We explain what to compare (fees, branch access, app language, requirements) so you choose.

**Can I send you my documents on WhatsApp?**
No — for your security we don't receive ID or immigration documents digitally. We talk through your situation, send you a checklist and check your documents in person before the bank appointment; you keep them. If a bank asks you to upload documents in its app or website, you do that yourself and we guide you step by step.
