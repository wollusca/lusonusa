### FAQs
**What is AIMA?**
AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo) is the Portuguese agency that took over the immigration functions of the former SEF in October 2023. It handles residence permits and renewals.

**I have a residence visa from the Portuguese embassy in Jakarta. What next?**
After entering Portugal you must obtain the residence permit from AIMA within your visa's validity. AIMA announced that holders of consular residence visas book their appointment online through its contact portal. Prepare your file before you request the slot.

**Do my Indonesian documents need an apostille?**
Usually yes. Indonesia is a party to the Apostille Convention (since 4 June 2022), so civil and criminal-record documents issued in Indonesia generally need an apostille and a certified Portuguese translation to be accepted [verify for your document type].

**Can you guarantee an appointment or approval?**
No one honestly can. AIMA alone decides and sets the calendar. What we can do is make sure your file is complete, so you don't lose your slot to a missing document.

**Has the law changed recently?**
Yes. Law 61/2025 amended the Foreigners' Act (Lei 23/2007) from 23 October 2025, including stricter rules for job-seeker visas and family reunification (generally after two years of legal residence, with exceptions) [verify]. The "manifestação de interesse" route closed in June 2024 [verify]. We check the current rules for every case.

**My application was refused. Can you appeal?**
We don't represent clients legally. We'll organise your documents and introduce you to a licensed partner lawyer or solicitor who can assess an appeal.

**Can I send you my documents on WhatsApp?**
No — for your security we don't receive ID or immigration documents digitally. We talk your case through, send you a checklist and review your documents in person at a meeting or on the appointment day; you keep them. Where the AIMA portal asks for a scan, you upload it yourself and we guide you step by step.

### Tanya jawab
**AIMA itu apa?**
AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo) adalah lembaga imigrasi Portugal yang menggantikan fungsi SEF sejak Oktober 2023. AIMA yang mengurus izin tinggal dan perpanjangannya.

**Saya sudah dapat visa tinggal dari Kedutaan Portugal di Jakarta. Lalu apa?**
Setelah tiba di Portugal, Anda harus mengurus izin tinggal di AIMA selama visa masih berlaku. Menurut AIMA, pemegang visa tinggal konsuler membuat jadwal secara online lewat portal kontak AIMA. Siapkan berkas dulu sebelum minta jadwal.

**Apakah dokumen dari Indonesia harus di-apostille?**
Umumnya ya. Indonesia sudah ikut Konvensi Apostille sejak 4 Juni 2022, jadi akta kelahiran, akta nikah, SKCK, dan dokumen sejenis biasanya perlu apostille plus terjemahan bahasa Portugis yang disertifikasi [verify sesuai jenis dokumen].

**Bisa jamin dapat jadwal atau pasti disetujui?**
Tidak ada yang bisa menjamin dengan jujur. Jadwal dan keputusan sepenuhnya di tangan AIMA. Tugas kami memastikan berkas Anda lengkap supaya kesempatan tidak hilang karena kurang satu dokumen.

**Katanya aturan imigrasi Portugal baru berubah?**
Betul. Lei 61/2025 mengubah UU Orang Asing (Lei 23/2007) mulai 23 Oktober 2025, antara lain aturan visa cari kerja yang lebih ketat dan reunifikasi keluarga yang umumnya baru bisa setelah 2 tahun tinggal legal, dengan beberapa pengecualian [verify]. Jalur "manifestação de interesse" sudah ditutup sejak Juni 2024 [verify]. Kami selalu cek aturan terbaru untuk tiap kasus.

**Permohonan saya ditolak. Bisa dibantu banding?**
Kami tidak mewakili klien secara hukum. Kami bantu rapikan dokumen Anda dan menghubungkan dengan mitra advogado/solicitador berlisensi yang bisa menilai peluang banding.

**Boleh saya kirim dokumen lewat WhatsApp?**
Tidak — demi keamanan Anda, kami tidak menerima dokumen identitas atau imigrasi secara digital. Kita bahas kasus Anda, kami kirim checklist, lalu dokumen dicek langsung saat bertemu atau di hari H; dokumen tetap Anda pegang. Kalau portal AIMA meminta scan, Anda sendiri yang mengunggahnya di portal, dan kami pandu langkah demi langkah.
