# Offers, Packages & Lead Tracking

**Source:** audit §2.8 (B1–B4) and §6.2–6.3. Replaces the current "€15–€30/hour" pathway and the "1–2 days of free research before a free call" promise (`app.js` `pathway.*`, `workflow.s2.*`).
**Prices:** none are set here. Every price is `[PRICE: owner to set]`. §3 gives a costing method so the owner can fill them in.
**Wording:** every package is *information and practical guidance based on public official information, plus in-person accompaniment and document coordination*. LUSONUSA is not a law firm and does not replace a lawyer. On the LUSO side this is **business guidance**, never legal, tax, investment or customs-brokerage advice. Legal acts, tax filings and customs declarations are done by licensed professionals we refer clients to (advogado/solicitador, notary, contabilista certificado, Indonesian law firm/notary, licensed tax consultant, customs broker). Government, notary and partner fees are always passed through at cost and shown separately.

**Document rule (owner decision, applies to every offer):** LUSONUSA does **not** receive ID or immigration documents (passports, residence permits, visas, civil-status certificates, student cards) by WhatsApp, email or upload. Remote work = conversation + checklists. Documents are checked **in person**, and the client keeps them. Where an official portal requires an upload (NISS form, AIMA portal, Indonesian e-Visa), the client uploads it themselves while we guide them.

**No recruitment:** LUSONUSA does not recruit, place or supply workers (that requires a licence in Portugal). Employers may only *refer their already-hired staff* to us for administrative guidance.

## 3. How to set the prices (method for the owner)

1. **Time per package.** Log hours for the first 3 clients of each package: prep, appointments, travel, messaging.
2. **Target rate.** Set an internal hourly cost rate, then multiply by a margin. Use separate targets per audience: NUSA (accessible) and LUSO (B2B market rate). Use competitor public prices as a sanity check. Big Indonesia setup agencies publish PT PMA packages in EN, and Portuguese relocation firms publish NIF/AIMA packages. Keep LUSO prices **clearly above** "€15–30/hour" territory (audit B3).
3. **Publish "from" prices** for every package once the first 3 cases are logged. Show inclusions and exclusions next to each price.
4. **Review quarterly.** Raise prices when the conversion rate on triage → paid exceeds ~50% *(rule of thumb, owner to adjust)*.

## 5. Lead-tracking scheme

### 5.1 WhatsApp prefill source tags

Every WhatsApp link carries a **ref tag at the end of the prefilled message** (WhatsApp strips UTMs, so the tag *is* the attribution):

```
Ref: LN-<SERVICE>-<LANG>-<PLACEMENT>[-<CAMPAIGN>]
```

| Part | Values |
|---|---|
| `LN` | Fixed prefix (LUSONUSA). Makes tags searchable in WhatsApp Business |
| `SERVICE` | `PMA` PT PMA · `VISA` Indonesia visa & KITAS · `VERIF` verification · `SRC` sourcing · `EXIM` export-import · `NIF` NIF & NISS · `AIMA` AIMA · `BANK` bank & arrival · `TRAD` translation · `STU` students & housing · `CONC` concierge add-on · `GEN` general/unknown · `MEA` Market Entry Assessment · `CASE` Case Check |
| `LANG` | `EN` · `PT` · `ID` (page language) |
| `PLACEMENT` | `HOME` homepage · `HUB` audience hub · `SVC` service page · `GDE` guide (append guide number from the calendar, e.g. `GDE01`) · `PRC` pricing · `CTC` contact page · `RES` resolver · `STK` mobile sticky bar · `GBP` Google Business Profile · `IG` Instagram · `LI` LinkedIn · `TT` TikTok · `NL` newsletter · `QR` printed QR / event · `PTN` partner referral (append partner code) |
| `CAMPAIGN` (optional) | e.g. `PPI20`, `CEPA26`, `UNI-ULISBOA` |

Examples:
- ID NIF guide, end CTA: `Ref: LN-NIF-ID-GDE22`
- PT PMA service page: `Ref: LN-PMA-PT-SVC`
- PPI webinar QR code: `Ref: LN-STU-ID-QR-PPI20`

**Prefill copy rule:** a natural sentence, no `[Name]`/`[details]` placeholders (audit C5). Examples:
- ID: `Halo LUSONUSA, saya butuh bantuan mengurus NIF & NISS di Portugal. Boleh tahu langkah dan biayanya? Ref: LN-NIF-ID-SVC`
- PT: `Olá LUSONUSA, gostaria de saber como abrir uma PT PMA na Indonésia. Podemos marcar uma chamada? Ref: LN-PMA-PT-SVC`
- EN: `Hi LUSONUSA, I'd like help with my AIMA appointment. What do you need from me? Ref: LN-AIMA-EN-SVC`

Analytics event (Plausible/Umami/GA4 behind consent): `wa_click` with props `service`, `lang`, `placement`. The same values as the tag allow site clicks to be matched with conversations started.

### 5.2 UTM conventions (for links *to* the site)

All lowercase, hyphens, no spaces.

| Param | Convention | Examples |
|---|---|---|

Rules: the GBP website link uses `?utm_source=gbp&utm_medium=organic-local&utm_campaign=gbp-<lang>` (one GBP link per language if the profile allows). Never put UTMs on internal links. Keep a shared sheet "UTM builder" with one row per link created.

### 5.3 Web intake form (B2B fallback + pre-triage)

Fields: name · email · WhatsApp (optional) · I am: *individual / business* · service (dropdown = SERVICE codes) · where are you now (PT / ID / other) · timeline (*< 1 month / 1–3 months / later*) · short description · consent checkbox (privacy policy `/privacy/`). Hidden fields: `lang`, `placement`, `utm_*`, `ref` (same tag format). **No file-upload field.** The form says: "Please don't send ID or immigration documents. We'll check them with you in person." Form tool decision: **owner** (Formspree/Tally/HubSpot free).

### 5.4 CRM stages

Tool: HubSpot free, Pipedrive, or a shared Notion/Google Sheet (**owner decision**). WhatsApp Business labels mirror the stages.

| # | Stage | Entry criteria | Owner action / SLA | WhatsApp label |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **New lead** | Any message/form with or without ref tag | Reply within 1 business day (within service hours). Log `ref`, language, service | `1-New` |
| 1 | **Triage booked** | 15-min call scheduled | Send intake form if not done | `2-Triage` |
| 2 | **Qualified** | Need fits a package; budget/timeline plausible | Send package + price, or offer Case Check / MEA | `3-Qualified` |
| 3 | **Proposal sent** | Written quote (package, inclusions, pass-through estimate) | Follow up at +2 and +7 days | `4-Proposal` |
| 4 | **Won** | Payment or signed engagement letter | Create case file. Agree document-handling consent | `5-Won` |
| 5 | **In delivery** | Work started | Weekly status to client | `6-Active` |
| 6 | **Delivered** | Outcome reached or package scope completed | Send summary + invoice for pass-throughs | `7-Done` |
| 7 | **Review requested** | 2–5 days after Delivered | Send review script (`off-page-playbook.md` §2). Ask for a referral | `8-Review` |

**Fields to record per lead:** date, name, audience (LUSO/NUSA), language, service code, ref tag (full), utm_source/medium/campaign (if form), stage, value (quoted, won), lost reason, review left (y/n), referral source.

**Monthly KPIs** (report alongside audit §4.4/§5.4): leads by `SERVICE`×`PLACEMENT`; triage→qualified %; qualified→won %; average won value by audience; time to first reply; reviews requested vs received; share of leads with a ref tag (target ≥ 80%).
