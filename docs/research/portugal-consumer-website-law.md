## Q1. Right of withdrawal (livre resolução) for consumer service contracts

### The rule

- **Diploma.** Decreto-Lei n.º 24/2014, de 14 de fevereiro, transposing Directive 2011/83/EU. The amending chain, per the consolidation listing [S], is: Lei 47/2014, DL 78/2018, DL 9/2021, DL 109-G/2021, Lei 16/2022 and **Lei 10/2023, de 3 de março** (Omnibus transposition, in force 2 April 2023).
  - Diploma: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/24-2014-572450
  - Consolidated text: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/2014-73222992
  - Lei 10/2023: https://files.dre.pt/1s/2023/03/04500/0001100039.pdf
  - I found no 2024–2026 national amendment specific to services. The Directive 2023/2673 transposition is **[unconfirmed]**; see the end of this section.
- **Period.** The consumer may withdraw within **14 days** without giving a reason and without costs, counted for services from the day the contract is concluded. **DL 24/2014 art. 10.º, n.º 1, al. a)** [S, cited in a CNIACC award].
- **If you omit the withdrawal information,** the period extends by **12 months**. **Art. 10.º, n.º 2** [K; also reported by secondary sources].
- **How the consumer withdraws.** Any unequivocal statement, or the model form in the **Anexo** (Parte B). **Art. 11.º** [K].
- **Refunds.** Refund within 14 days of the withdrawal. **Art. 12.º** [S, secondary].
- **Burden of proof.** The trader must prove it gave the pre-contractual information. **Art. 4.º, n.º 8** [S]. This matters for retention (Q4): keep the evidence.

### How the provider protects itself when work starts within the 14 days

1. **Express request on a durable medium (art. 15.º, n.º 1)** [S]. If the consumer wants the service to begin during the withdrawal period, the provider must require an express request on a durable medium. An email or WhatsApp message that the consumer sends and that you keep qualifies: the legal definition of durable medium is open, covering "papel, chave USB, …, disco rígido" [S].
2. **Proportional payment if they withdraw later (art. 15.º, n.º 2)** [S]. If the consumer withdraws after making that request, they must pay "um montante proporcional ao que foi efetivamente prestado até ao momento da comunicação da resolução, em relação ao conjunto das prestações previstas no contrato".
   - The amount is calculated on the total contract price. If that price is excessive, it is calculated on the market value of what was provided [S, from a doctrinal source citing art. 15].
   - **The consumer owes nothing for work done in the period if you did not inform them of this payment duty before the contract** [S]. This is why the Terms and the quote must say it.
3. **Loss of the right once the service is fully performed (art. 17.º, n.º 1, al. a))** [S]. The right of withdrawal does not apply to service contracts once the service has been **fully performed**, provided that:
   - (i) performance began with the consumer's **prior express consent**, and
   - (ii) the consumer **acknowledged** that they lose the right once the contract is fully performed.
   - If consent or acknowledgement is missing, the exception does not work [S].
   - Typical case: a single accompaniment appointment, done and finished on day 3, means no withdrawal after that.
4. **Required information (art. 4.º, n.º 1)** [S/K]. Before the contract, in clear and comprehensible language, you must give:
   - identity (name), **physical address**, phone and email (al. a)) [S];
   - the identity and address of any professional acting on the provider's behalf (al. b)) [S];
   - the main characteristics of the service;
   - the total price including taxes, or how it is calculated;
   - payment and performance arrangements;
   - complaint handling;
   - the conditions, deadline and procedure for withdrawal, plus the model form;
   - that the consumer pays a proportional amount if they withdraw after requesting early start;
   - where there is no right of withdrawal (art. 17), that fact;
   - duration;
   - access to ADR.

   The exact alínea letters after (b) changed in 2021/2023 **[unconfirmed]**. Put all of them in the Terms or on the quote.
5. **Confirmation on durable medium (art. 6.º)** [S, secondary]. Confirm the distance contract within a reasonable time, at the latest before the service starts. Secondary sources state five days. Practical answer: send the written quote/acceptance summary by email or WhatsApp before you start.

### B2B?

**No.** DL 24/2014 protects "consumidores", meaning individuals acting outside their trade, business or profession (art. 3.º definitions) [K]. Portuguese companies buying Indonesia-entry guidance have **no statutory withdrawal right**. Your Terms can exclude it for business clients. Have business clients confirm they act for business purposes and give a company NIF.

### Contracts concluded face to face?

- **There is no establishment open to the public, so a deal agreed in person is very likely a contract "celebrado fora do estabelecimento comercial"** (art. 3.º definitions: contracts concluded with the simultaneous physical presence of trader and consumer in a place that is not the trader's business premises) [K]. The **same 14-day withdrawal right applies**.
- Such contracts **must be in writing, in Portuguese, containing the art. 4 information, on pain of nullity**, and the consumer must receive a signed copy or confirmation (on paper, or on another durable medium if they agree). **Art. 9.º, n.ºs 1–2** [S].
- So the only case **outside** DL 24/2014 is a contract concluded at the trader's own premises. You have none.
- **Practical rule:** never "close" a consumer deal orally. Every deal gets a written quote and a written "Aceito" (accepted) from the client, even when it was agreed at a meeting.

### Wording for the Terms (Portuguese is mandatory for consumers; English/Indonesian versions may follow)

> **Direito de livre resolução (consumidores).** Se é consumidor, tem o direito de resolver este contrato no prazo de 14 dias a contar da sua celebração, sem necessidade de indicar qualquer motivo e sem custos, nos termos dos artigos 10.º a 17.º do Decreto-Lei n.º 24/2014. Para exercer este direito, envie-nos uma declaração inequívoca (por exemplo, por e-mail para [email] ou WhatsApp para [n.º]) ou utilize o modelo de formulário abaixo. Basta que a comunicação seja enviada antes do fim do prazo. Reembolsamos todos os pagamentos recebidos no prazo de 14 dias a contar da data em que formos informados da sua decisão, pelo mesmo meio de pagamento, salvo acordo em contrário.
>
> **Início do serviço durante o prazo.** Se nos pedir expressamente que o serviço comece antes de terminado o prazo de 14 dias, e depois resolver o contrato, pagará um montante proporcional ao serviço efetivamente prestado até nos comunicar a resolução, calculado sobre o preço total acordado (art. 15.º do DL 24/2014). Se o serviço for integralmente prestado dentro do prazo, a pedido seu, perde o direito de livre resolução (art. 17.º, n.º 1, al. a)).
>
> **Clientes empresariais.** O direito de livre resolução não se aplica a contratos celebrados com empresas ou profissionais para fins da sua atividade.

**Express request + acknowledgement.** The client sends this message in WhatsApp/email; keep it.

> "Peço expressamente que o serviço [descrição] comece antes do fim do prazo de 14 dias de livre resolução. Tomo conhecimento de que, se resolver o contrato, pagarei o valor proporcional ao serviço já prestado, e de que perco o direito de livre resolução quando o serviço estiver totalmente prestado."

**Model withdrawal form** (Anexo, Parte B, DL 24/2014; reproduce it in the Terms) [K: standard Directive Annex I(B) text]:

> Para: [nome do profissional], [endereço], [e-mail]
> Pela presente comunico que resolvo o meu contrato de prestação do seguinte serviço: [ ]
> Contratado em: [data]
> Nome do consumidor: [ ]
> Endereço do consumidor: [ ]
> Assinatura (apenas se em papel): [ ]
> Data: [ ]

**Status: (a) Mandatory now, with real enforcement risk.**
- ASAE enforces this, and fines exist under the diploma's contraordenações article.
- The bigger private risk: if the information is missing, the client can withdraw for up to 12 months **and pay nothing for work already done**.

**Directive 2023/2673 "withdrawal function/button".** It inserts art. 11a into Dir. 2011/83, transposition was due 19 Dec 2025, and it applies from **19 June 2026** [S, secondary].
- It covers distance contracts concluded **through an online interface** (website/app).
- Contracts concluded by WhatsApp/email do not run through your own online interface. This reading is **[unconfirmed]**, but it is the natural one.
- I could not confirm the Portuguese transposing act **[unconfirmed]**. If you add an online order/booking form, add a "Resolver contrato aqui" function.
- Directive: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/PT/TXT/PDF/?uri=OJ%3AL_202302673

## Q2. Identification duties on the website: is "Lisboa" + email enough?

### Rules

- **DL 7/2004 (e-commerce), art. 10.º, n.º 1** [S]. Information-society service providers must make permanently, easily and directly accessible online:
  - (a) name or company name;
  - (b) **geographic address** ("endereço geográfico") where established, and an **email address allowing direct contact**;
  - (c) registrations in public registers;
  - (d) **tax identification number (NIF)**.
- **Art. 10.º, n.º 3:** regulated professions add title, State, professional body and rules. This does not apply here.
- **Art. 10.º, n.º 4:** costs beyond telecoms must be stated.
- **Sanction:** omission is a contraordenação, **€2,500–€50,000** [S].
- Diploma: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/7-2004-240775. Copy at ANACOM: https://www.anacom.pt/render.jsp?contentId=952094.
- A website that advertises paid services is an "information society service" even without online checkout. The provider here is the website operator (the founder) [K].
- **DL 24/2014 art. 4.º, n.º 1, al. a)** [S]: identity, **"endereço físico onde está estabelecido"**, phone and email.
  - DL 78/2018 (in force 1 Jan 2019) requires the **physical address to appear in the contract proposal and in the contract** [S, law-firm alerts].
  - Reported fines for omission: €400–€2,000 for individuals, €2,500–€25,000 for companies [S, secondary].
  - DL 78/2018: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/78-2018-116673878
- **GDPR art. 13(1)(a)** [K]: "the identity and the contact details of the controller". GDPR itself does not demand a postal address, but the Portuguese rules above do.
  - GDPR: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj

### Answer

**Publishing only "Lisboa" is not compliant.** Both DL 7/2004 art. 10 and DL 24/2014 art. 4 require a full geographic/physical address (street, number, postcode, city).

**Minimum identification block on the website:**

- Operator: Aditya Putra (name as on NIF), trading as LUSONUSA.
- Address: full postal address.
  - A **domiciliation / virtual office / mailbox service address** where you can actually receive mail is commonly used instead of a home address.
  - Whether a pure P.O. box satisfies "endereço geográfico" is **[unconfirmed]**. A domiciliation address with a street is safer.
- Email + phone/WhatsApp number.
- NIF (Portuguese, personal).
- If the Indonesian company is a contracting party, add its name, registration number, address and tax ID.
- For each professional who contracts with clients, their name and NIF, and their address on the quote/contract.

**Status: (a) Mandatory now**, with real fines. It is cheap to fix.

## Q3. Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico (LRE) and ADR information

### LRE rule

- **Diploma.** DL 156/2005, de 15 de setembro, as amended notably by **DL 74/2017, de 21 de junho**, which created the electronic book. Regulated by **Portaria 201-A/2017**.
  - Consolidated text: https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_mostra_articulado.php?nid=737&tabela=leis
  - DL 74/2017: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/74-2017-107541411
  - Portaria 201-A/2017: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/portaria/201-a-2017-107614162
- **Physical book (art. 2.º, n.º 1)** is only for fixed or permanent establishments with contact with the public [S]. **You do not need one.**
- **Electronic format (art. 2.º, n.º 2)** applies to suppliers of goods and providers of services, whether they act through an establishment or **only through distance channels** [S: ASF, AMT, DGAE guidance].
  - ASF: https://www.asf.com.pt/w/livro-de-reclama%C3%A7%C3%B5es-em-formato-digital
  - DGAE FAQ: https://areadocomerciante.dgae.gov.pt/documentacao1/documentos/faq_livro_reclamacoes-pdf.aspx
- **Website duty.** Providers with a website must show access to the LRE platform **"em local visível e de forma destacada"**: **art. 5.º-B** [S: ERS alert citing the article]. Providers without a website need an email address for receiving complaints [S].
  - The law does not prescribe a format. The AMT/DGC icon linking to https://www.livroreclamacoes.pt is voluntary but standard [S].
- **Answering complaints.** AMT guidance: 15 working days. The health sector has its own 10-day rule [S, sectoral guidance]. Check the general deadline in the consolidated text **[unconfirmed]**.
- **Fines.** €150–€15,000 depending on the infraction [S, DGAE].
- **Cost and steps.**
  - Registration on livroreclamacoes.pt is **free**, with NIF and authentication via Chave Móvel Digital / Cartão de Cidadão or the Portal das Finanças password, and includes an initial allocation of electronic complaint sheets [S, secondary].
  - Extra packs are sold by INCM (about €10 for 25 sheets in 2023-era sources). The 2017 Portaria figure was €9.88 [S, secondary; current price **unconfirmed**].
  - **One electronic book per provider**, regardless of the number of establishments [S, Portaria].

### Who needs it here

- **Each self-employed professional who contracts with consumers is a separate "prestador de serviços".** The book attaches to the provider and its NIF, so **each needs their own registration** [K: the logic of art. 2 and the Portaria's "one book per provider"]. Being self-employed without premises does not exempt you [S].
- **Founder.** If he only runs the website and never contracts, the obligation attaches to the contracting professionals. If he contracts or invoices personally, he registers too. Registration is free, so **register anyway**.
- **Foreign (Indonesian) company invoicing Portuguese consumers.** Whether DL 156/2005 reaches a provider not established in Portugal is **[unconfirmed]**. The diploma's logic covers providers "acting" in Portuguese territory. If the company invoices PT consumers for services performed in Lisbon, assume yes. Better: avoid invoicing consumers through the foreign company (see Q8).
- **Website link.** Put the LRE icon/link in the footer, with a line such as: "Cada profissional da rede tem o seu próprio Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico (pesquise pelo nome ou NIF indicado no orçamento)."

### ADR (Lei 144/2015, art. 18.º)

- **Rule (art. 18.º)** [S]. Suppliers and providers established in Portugal must inform consumers of the ADR entities available to them or to which they adhere, **including each entity's website**.
  - The information must be clear, comprehensible and easily accessible **on the provider's website (if any) and in the contract terms / general conditions**, or on another durable medium.
  - Joining an ADR entity is not compulsory; only the information duty is [S].
  - ASAE enforces it [S].
  - Fine amounts: **[unconfirmed]** (art. 23).
  - Consolidated text: https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/leis/lei_busca_art_velho.php?nid=2425&artigonum=2425A0018&n_versao=1&so_miolo=S
  - ASAE FAQ: https://www.asae.gov.pt/perguntas-frequentes1/area-economica/resolucao-alternativa-de-litigios-de-consumo.aspx
- **Arbitration is necessary at the consumer's option** for disputes **up to €5,000**: Lei 24/96 art. 14.º as amended by **Lei 63/2019** [S]. If a consumer files at a consumer arbitration centre, the professional **must** take part.
  - Lei 63/2019: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/63-2019-123962147
- **Competent entities for Lisbon** [S, CNIACC awards]:
  - **CACCL – Centro de Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo de Lisboa** (Área Metropolitana de Lisboa; up to €5,000): https://www.centroarbitragemlisboa.pt
  - **CNIACC** (residual/national, "atuação supletiva"): https://www.cniacc.pt
- **EU ODR platform.** It was abolished by **Regulation (EU) 2024/3228**, and the duty to link it ended **20 July 2025** [S]. **Remove any ODR link.**
  - Regulation: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/3228/oj/eng

**Wording (footer + Terms):**

> **Resolução alternativa de litígios.** Em caso de litígio de consumo, o consumidor pode recorrer a: Centro de Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo de Lisboa (www.centroarbitragemlisboa.pt) ou Centro Nacional de Informação e Arbitragem de Conflitos de Consumo – CNIACC (www.cniacc.pt). Mais informações em www.consumidor.gov.pt. Livro de Reclamações Eletrónico: www.livroreclamacoes.pt.

**Status: (a) Mandatory now** for both the LRE and the ADR information. They are free and take minutes, and they are common ASAE checks.

## Q4. Data retention (GDPR art. 5(1)(e)) and tax / limitation periods

### Rules

- **GDPR art. 5(1)(e):** keep data no longer than necessary. **Art. 13(2)(a):** state the period or the criteria.
- **Lei 58/2019, art. 21.º** (national GDPR law) [S, secondary]. The period is the one fixed by law or regulation; failing that, the period necessary for the purpose.
  - It is widely read to allow keeping data needed to prove contractual obligations **until the relevant claims prescribe** [S/K; exact wording of n.º 2 **unconfirmed**].
  - Lei 58/2019 (official text): https://comarcas.tribunais.org.pt/comarcas/pdf2/evora/pdf/Lei_n_58_2019_08_agosto.pdf
- **Tax: CIVA art. 52.º.**
  - VAT taxpayers must archive and keep books, records and supporting documents (invoices) for **10 years** (current wording, in force 1 July 2025 per OCC/informador) [S].
  - The period extends to the end of the tax-assessment limitation period where a right with a longer period is exercised [S].
  - CIVA: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/legislacao-consolidada/decreto-lei/2008-34500675
  - OCC note: https://www.occ.pt/pt-pt/noticias/iva-arquivo-de-faturas-e-outros-documentos
  - The 10-year rule also applies to VAT-exempt (art. 53) taxpayers, since they remain sujeitos passivos [K]. **CIRS:** the IRS code has its own bookkeeping rules for category B, and I could not confirm the specific CIRS article **[unconfirmed]**. Follow CIVA art. 52 (10 years). Note that invoices issued on the Portal das Finanças are also held by AT.
- **Código Comercial art. 40.º:** merchants keep correspondence, documents and books for 10 years [S, secondary]. This is relevant only if acting as a "comerciante" or company.
- **Código Civil limitation periods** [S/K]:
  - **Art. 309.º:** ordinary period 20 years. This covers, for example, a client's contractual damages claim for a badly performed service [K].
  - **Art. 310.º:** 5 years for periodic payments [K]. Not really relevant.
  - **Art. 317.º, al. c):** **2-year presumptive prescription** for credits for "serviços prestados no exercício de profissões liberais" and related expenses [S]. It rests on a presumption of payment and is rebuttable only in limited ways (arts. 312–314) [S].
    - Whether translators/consultants count as "profissões liberais" depends on the nature of the service [S, divided case law].
    - Practical effect: your **fee claims** may become hard to enforce after 2 years.
  - **Art. 498.º:** 3 years for non-contractual (tort) claims [K].
  - Art. 317 text: https://informador.pt/?p=156348

### Recommendations

| Data | Suggested retention | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry that does not become a client (WhatsApp/email) | **6–12 months** after last contact, then delete. | Legitimate interest (pre-contract steps, art. 6(1)(b)); nothing to prove later. |
| Client communications (full chat/email history) | **Delete the bulk 12–24 months after the service ends.** Keep the **contract evidence pack** (quote, acceptance, express request/acknowledgement, delivery confirmation, payment proof) **5 years**, and until any dispute or claim is closed. | Burden of proof on info duties (DL 24/2014 art. 4(8)). Art. 17(1)(f) GDPR exception for legal claims. 5 years is a proportionate compromise; the 20-year art. 309 period would be hard to justify for everything. |
| Identity documents / documents sent unprompted | **Do not store.** Delete documents sent unprompted at once and tell the sender. For translation, keep source docs only until delivery plus a short complaint window (e.g. 30 days), unless the client asks otherwise. | Data minimisation (art. 5(1)(c)). Lower breach risk. |
| Invoices / accounting | **10 years**, kept by whoever issues the invoice (each professional; the Indonesian company under Indonesian law plus any PT obligations). | CIVA art. 52.º. |
| Website logs / contact form | 30–90 days. | Security legitimate interest. |

**Can retention be shorter?** Yes, except where a law fixes a minimum (tax: 10 years for invoices).

**Risks of very short retention:**
- You may be unable to prove the express request/acknowledgement, which can mean a full refund with no payment for work done.
- You may be unable to prove you gave the art. 4 information, or that the client approved extra hours.
- You may be unable to defend an arbitration (CACCL) or court claim, or to rebut a GDPR complaint.

Keep the small evidence pack, not everything.

**Status:** a retention policy stated in the privacy notice is **(a) mandatory now** (art. 13). The exact periods are your choice. **Cookies** (Lei 41/2004 art. 5, prior consent for non-essential cookies) [S, secondary] are **(a) mandatory now if** you run analytics, pixels or YouTube/Maps embeds. Otherwise there is nothing to do.

**Other privacy items for now:**
- Name the processors: WhatsApp/Meta (WhatsApp Business terms), email host, website host.
- Disclose transfers outside the EEA. Meta and US hosts rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (verify each vendor). **Indonesia has no adequacy decision**, so sharing client data with the Indonesian company or Indonesian partners needs SCCs (art. 46) or an art. 49 derogation (e.g., necessary for the client's contract) [K].
- Explain that enquiries are forwarded to the specific professional, who then becomes a separate controller for the work.
- State the right to complain to the **CNPD** (www.cnpd.pt).

## Q5. Liability within a network under one brand

### Legal position

- **Joint (solidary) liability is not presumed in civil law.** It arises only from law or agreement: **Código Civil art. 513.º** [K]. In commercial acts, co-obligors are presumed solidary: **Código Comercial art. 100.º** [K]. The founder should avoid becoming a co-obligor at all.
- **If the brand operator is the contracting party**, he is liable for the professionals he uses to perform as if the act were his own: **CC art. 800.º** (liability for auxiliaries in performance) [K]. **This is the main risk.** If the client reasonably believes they contracted with "LUSONUSA", the operator may be treated as the contractor.
- **Apparent representation (representação aparente).** Portuguese courts apply **DL 178/86 (agency contract), art. 23.º** by analogy. A third party is protected where (1) objective reasons justify their trust, (2) they acted in good faith, and (3) **the principal contributed to creating that trust** [S, TRP and TRC rulings].
  - Ruling: https://trc.pt/ap3841074tbavrc1/
  - A single brand, "our team", one WhatsApp number and payment to the founder all contribute to that trust.
- **Consumer information duties.**
  - DL 24/2014 art. 4.º, n.º 1, al. a)–b) requires the identity and address of the provider **and of any professional acting on its behalf** [S].
  - DL 57/2008 (unfair commercial practices) treats misleading or omitted information about the trader's identity as a misleading practice [K; exact article **unconfirmed**].
  - Lei 24/96 art. 8.º is the general duty to inform [K].
  - Hiding who the real provider is creates **both** regulatory and liability risk.

### Wording and practice that reduce risk

1. **Terms/footer:**
   > "LUSONUSA é uma marca e um sítio web operados por Aditya Putra (NIF …), que apresenta uma rede de profissionais independentes. Cada serviço é contratado diretamente com o profissional identificado no orçamento, que o presta em nome próprio, emite a fatura-recibo e é o único responsável pela sua execução. O operador do sítio não é parte nesse contrato, salvo quando o orçamento o indique expressamente."
2. **Every quote and invoice names the contracting professional:** full name, NIF, address, email, and "presta o serviço em nome próprio e é responsável por ele".
3. **The client accepts the quote of that named professional** before work starts.
4. **Payments go to the professional's own account**, not the founder's.
5. Each professional communicates in their own name, e.g. "Sou a X, tradutora independente da rede LUSONUSA". Ideally use their own number, or at least sign messages.
6. The website "Team" page states that members are independent.
7. Agree a short **network agreement** between the founder and the professionals covering:
   - brand use;
   - that each professional is liable for their own work;
   - each professional holding their own LRE and invoicing;
   - each being an independent controller for client data;
   - indemnity to the founder.
8. The founder's own work (e.g., Indonesia-entry consulting) is quoted in his or his company's name, so those clients know exactly who is liable.
9. Consider professional liability insurance for the translator first (higher-risk errors).

**Status: (a) Do now.** It costs nothing and is the single most important structural protection.

## Q6. Late payment, deposits and cancellation

### Rules

- **Consumers (civil law).**
  - Delay in a money obligation gives rise to compensation equal to **interest from the date of default**: **CC art. 806.º** [K].
  - Default arises automatically when there is a fixed due date: **art. 805.º, n.º 2, al. a)** [K].
  - Rate: **civil legal interest 4% per year**, under **CC art. 559.º + Portaria 291/2003** [K; still the rate in 2026 per my knowledge **[unconfirmed for 2026]**].
- **B2B (commercial transactions).**
  - **DL 62/2013** applies to commercial transactions and **excludes contracts with consumers**: **art. 2.º, n.º 2, al. a)** [S, TRC ruling].
  - Creditors get **€40 fixed recovery costs** without reminder: **art. 7.º** [S, TRG ruling]. Reasonable extra costs may also be claimed [S].
  - Commercial default rates, 2nd half 2026, per **Aviso n.º 16623/2026/2 (DR 2.ª série, 3 July 2026)** [S]:
    - **9.40%** for commercial companies' credits (Código Comercial art. 102.º §3);
    - **10.40%** for transactions under DL 62/2013 (§5).
  - Aviso: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/aviso/16623-2026-1143256936
  - Whether a self-employed individual counts as an "empresa comercial" for the 9.40% rate is debatable [K]. DL 62/2013 covers "empresas", which includes liberal professionals in the Directive's sense [K]. Use the DL 62/2013 rate for B2B.
- **Unfair terms: DL 446/85 (cláusulas contratuais gerais).** Your Terms are general conditions, so it applies.
  - **Art. 19.º, al. c):** **penalty clauses disproportionate to the damage** are prohibited (relatively, judged against the standard deal). A court struck a 50% retention on cancellation where cancellation caused no real loss [S, STJ 2005].
  - **Art. 22.º** adds further relatively prohibited clauses in consumer relations, e.g. al. a) excessive notice periods and al. b) free termination by the drafter [S, case law].
  - **Art. 21.º:** absolutely prohibited consumer clauses [K].
  - Lei 32/2021 added font-size/legibility rules (minimum 11 pt / 2.5 mm, spacing ≥1.15) [K, **unconfirmed** alínea]. Format the Terms accordingly.
  - Lei 10/2023 strengthened fines for using prohibited clauses [S].
  - Case law: https://www.pgdlisboa.pt/jurel/stj_mostra_doc.php?nid=11218
- **CC art. 812.º:** courts can reduce manifestly excessive penalty clauses [K].
- **Withdrawal overlay:** within 14 days, DL 24/2014 overrides any cancellation fee. Only the art. 15 pro-rata amount may be charged.

### Recommended wording

> **Preço e orçamento.** Antes de iniciarmos, o profissional responsável envia um orçamento escrito com o âmbito, o preço total (com IVA incluído, ou com menção de isenção – art. 53.º do CIVA) e o prazo. O serviço só começa depois da sua aceitação escrita.
>
> **Adiantamento.** É pedido um adiantamento de 50% do preço na aceitação do orçamento. O restante é pago no prazo de 14 dias após a entrega/conclusão do serviço.
>
> **Cancelamento.** (i) Se for consumidor, tem sempre o direito de livre resolução de 14 dias descrito acima. (ii) Após esse prazo, ou se for cliente empresarial: se cancelar antes de o trabalho começar, devolvemos a totalidade do adiantamento; se o trabalho já tiver começado, devolvemos o adiantamento deduzido apenas das horas efetivamente trabalhadas (à tarifa horária indicada no orçamento) e de despesas já pagas a terceiros (por exemplo, emolumentos notariais), com discriminação por escrito. Se o valor devido exceder o adiantamento, a diferença é faturada.
>
> **Trabalho adicional.** Trabalho fora do âmbito do orçamento só é realizado após o informarmos previamente por escrito do seu custo e de o aceitar. É faturado separadamente. Se estimarmos que as horas previstas serão ultrapassadas, pedimos a sua autorização antes de continuar; sem autorização, não são cobradas horas além das orçamentadas.
>
> **Atraso no pagamento.** Consumidores: em caso de atraso, são devidos juros de mora à taxa legal em vigor (art. 806.º do Código Civil). Clientes empresariais: juros de mora à taxa aplicável às transações comerciais e indemnização de 40 € por custos de cobrança (DL 62/2013).

Avoid in consumer terms:
- flat "50% non-refundable" deposits;
- fixed cancellation penalties unrelated to work done;
- interest above the legal rate;
- unilateral price changes.

**Status: (a) Mandatory now** once you take deposits. Consumer-terms compliance is enforceable via the DL 446/85 inhibitory actions and fines, and in arbitration.

## Q7. Reserved legal acts: Lei 49/2004 replaced by Lei 10/2024

### Status in 2026

- **Lei 49/2004 was repealed by Lei n.º 10/2024, de 19 de janeiro**, the Regime Jurídico dos Atos de Advogados e Solicitadores [S].
  - It followed the reform of professional associations (Lei 12/2023, amending Lei 2/2013, and the new Estatuto da Ordem dos Advogados) [K].
  - The OA lists entry into force as 1 Jan 2024 [S, OA page]. **Check against the DR text, since the law is dated 19 Jan.**
  - Lei 10/2024: https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/10-2024-837135330
  - Lei 10/2024 (official PDF via OA): https://portal.oa.pt/media/142630/lei-n%C2%BA-10-2024-de-19-01-regime-juridico-dos-atos-de-advogados-e-solicitadores.pdf
  - Lei 49/2004 (repealed): https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/lei/49-2004-479604
- **No later amendment found.**
  - The OA's Conselho Geral has published a proposal to amend Lei 10/2024, e.g. to make procuradoria ilícita a public crime. I found **no evidence it was enacted** as of Oct 2026 **[unconfirmed]**.
  - In July 2025 the OA published a regulation creating a national commission against procuradoria ilícita [S]. Expect more active enforcement.
  - OA proposal: https://portal.oa.pt/media/144268/proposta-de-alteracao-legislativa-da-lei-atos-proprios.pdf

### What is reserved (as reported from the text)

- **Mandato forense** (representing clients in court) is **exclusive** to advogados and solicitadores: **art. 4.º** [S].
- Also listed as acts proper to lawyers/solicitadores [S, OA summary of art. 4]:
  - **consulta jurídica**;
  - **drafting contracts** and acts preparing the formation, amendment or termination of legal transactions;
  - **mandate in administrative or tax complaints/challenges** ("reclamação ou impugnação de atos administrativos ou tributários");
  - debt-collection negotiation.
- These are acts proper to the professions **only when done in the interest of third parties and as a professional activity** (art. 4.º, n.º 5) [S].
- **Definition of consulta jurídica (art. 6.º, n.º 1)** [S]: "atividade de aconselhamento jurídico que consiste na interpretação e aplicação de normas jurídicas mediante solicitação de terceiro".
- **Who else may give consulta jurídica (art. 6.º, n.º 2)** [S, quoted in OA Lisbon Parecer 28/PP/2023-C]: notários, agentes de execução, and **licenciados em Direito** (law graduates).
  - Parecer: https://www.lexpoint.pt/Fileget.aspx?FileId=53708
  - The consumer must be told that, if a dispute arises, only an advogado or solicitador may represent them in court [S].
  - So the 2024 reform **opened consulta jurídica to law graduates**, but **not to non-lawyers**: a criminology graduate was held not authorised [S].
- **Information given by public administration** about its own competences is not consulta jurídica [S, OA summary].
- **Crime.** Practising **exclusive** acts in breach of art. 4 is punishable with **up to 1 year in prison or up to 120 days' fine** (prosecution depends on a complaint). Advertising such acts by unauthorised persons is an administrative offence [S]. The article number for the crime is **[unconfirmed]**. The OA summaries are inconsistent about which acts are "exclusive" versus merely "proper", so verify against the DR text.

### Application to LUSONUSA (assuming no team member is a law graduate)

- **Allowed:**
  - general information on how public procedures work, taken from official sources (AIMA, AT, Segurança Social, bank requirements);
  - document checklists;
  - booking help;
  - **accompanying the client in person and interpreting**;
  - helping fill forms with the client's own data;
  - translation.
  - Explaining "what the AIMA website says the steps are" is not "interpretation and application of legal norms to your case".
- **Not allowed:**
  - telling a client what their legal position is ("you qualify for art. X visa", "you can appeal on these grounds");
  - drafting contracts, powers of attorney or legal letters for clients;
  - **acting as the client's representative in an administrative complaint or appeal** against AIMA/AT decisions;
  - advertising "legal assistance", "legal advice" or "immigration lawyer-like" services.
- **Business guidance for PT companies entering Indonesia.** Foreign (Indonesian) law guidance is a grey area: Lei 10/2024 concerns acts in PT, and its scope for foreign-law advice is **[unconfirmed]**. Present it as commercial/market guidance and refer legal questions to Indonesian counsel.

**Wording:**

> "Prestamos informação prática baseada em fontes oficiais públicas e acompanhamento presencial. Não prestamos consulta jurídica nem representamos clientes perante tribunais ou em reclamações/recursos administrativos. Sempre que a sua situação exija aconselhamento jurídico, recomendamos um advogado ou solicitador, que podemos ajudar a identificar."

**Status: (a) Mandatory now.** It is criminal-law exposure, and the OA is actively hunting procuradoria ilícita.

## Q8. Other day-one obligations vs. things that can wait

### Must do now

1. **Tax start-up for each professional.**
   - Each professional files a "declaração de início de atividade" (CIVA art. 31.º; CIRS art. 112.º [K]).
   - Issues **faturas-recibo** for every service via the Portal das Finanças. Invoice duty and content: CIVA arts. 29.º and 36.º [K].
   - VAT exemption: **CIVA art. 53.º** exemption up to **€15,000** turnover (prior year), lost when exceeding €18,750 in the current year. The rules were revised by DL 35/2025 from 1 July 2025 [S, AT Newsletter Jan 2026].
     - AT newsletter: https://info.portaldasfinancas.gov.pt/pt/at/Divulgacao/publicacoes_internas/Newsletter_AT/Documents/Newsletter-39-janeiro-2026.pdf
   - Register with Segurança Social as trabalhador independente.
2. **Founder invoicing through the Indonesian company.** A founder **resident in Lisbon performing services in Lisbon** through a foreign company raises:
   - PT tax residence/IRS questions;
   - possible permanent establishment;
   - VAT place-of-supply questions (CIVA art. 6.º).
   This is **[unconfirmed]** and needs an accountant (contabilista certificado) **before** the first such invoice. Simplest compliant path: invoice PT clients as a PT self-employed person, and use the Indonesian company only for work performed or contracted in Indonesia.
3. **Prices.**
   - Consumers must see the **total price including taxes** before contracting (DL 24/2014 art. 4) [S/K].
   - DL 138/90 (price indication; art. 10 for services: prices displayed where services are offered) [S] was amended by Lei 10/2023 [S].
   - On the website, "a partir de X € (IVA incluído)" or "isento de IVA – art. 53.º CIVA". Never show net-of-VAT prices to consumers.
   - DL 162/99 (amending DL 138/90): https://diariodarepublica.pt/dr/detalhe/decreto-lei/162-1999-321723
4. **Language.** Consumer information and contracts must be in **Portuguese**. Off-premises contracts are null otherwise (DL 24/2014 art. 9.º, n.º 1) [S]. DL 238/86 is the general rule [K]. Bilingual PT/EN/ID is fine; Portuguese governs.
5. **Unfair commercial practices (DL 57/2008).**
   - No misleading claims: "official", "guaranteed approval", "sworn translation" or "tradução juramentada".
   - **Portugal has no sworn-translator status.** Translations are certified by notaries, advogados, solicitadores, conservadores, consulates or recognised chambers of commerce. A suitable translator declares before them that the translation is faithful (Código do Notariado; DL 237/2001) [S, DGES].
     - DGES FAQ: https://www.dges.gov.pt/ost/kb/faq.php?id=242
   - Say "tradução certificada (certificação perante notário/advogado)".
6. **Items covered above:** LRE + ADR info; full address/NIF; privacy notice; cookie consent if using trackers; written quotes; withdrawal info.

### Can wait (low risk while tiny)

- **GDPR art. 30 records.** Firms with fewer than 250 staff are exempt unless processing is not occasional. Client processing is arguably regular, so keep a one-page record anyway (low effort).
- **DPO:** not required.
- **Processor contracts (art. 28).** Rely on vendors' standard DPAs (Google/Microsoft/Meta). Just keep a list.
- **European Accessibility Act (DL 82/2022, applicable 28 June 2025).** Microenterprises providing services are exempt [K, **unconfirmed** article]. Good practice only.
- **Directive 2023/2673 withdrawal button.** Only if you add online contracting (see Q1).
- **Trademark registration (INPI)**, professional liability insurance (sensible for the translator), and formal company incorporation.
- **Network agreement among professionals.** Do it soon; it has no regulatory deadline.
