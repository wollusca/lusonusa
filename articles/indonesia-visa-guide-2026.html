1. Indonesia's visa index at a glance

Indonesia no longer uses labels such as "B211A" or "C317". Each visa now has an index code: B for visa on arrival, C for single-entry visit visas, D for multiple-entry visit visas and E for limited-stay permits (ITAS, commonly called KITAS). Applications are made online through the Directorate General of Immigration's e-Visa portal.

Indonesian immigration law separates visiting (tourism, meetings, negotiations) from working (employment or being paid by an Indonesian entity). Choose the visa that matches what you will actually do:

2. C2 business visit visa (the replacement for the old B211A)

If you need more than the 30 days of a visa on arrival, the C2 business visit visa is the usual choice. It gives a 60-day stay and can be extended inside Indonesia check current extension limit; immigration offices have described this as up to two further 60-day extensions.

Allowed: meetings, negotiations, trade fairs, supplier and factory visits, feasibility studies. Not allowed: employment or receiving payment from an Indonesian company.

What you will usually need

  • A passport valid for at least 6 months.
  • A letter or invitation from the Indonesian company or government body you are visiting, explaining your relationship with them.
  • Recent bank statements showing enough funds for your stay (agencies commonly cite about USD 2,000).
  • A return or onward ticket.

The official C2 page listed a government fee (PNBP) of IDR 2,000,000 at the time of review. Check the C2 page on imigrasi.go.id before you apply.

3. D2 multiple-entry business visa

The D2 visa covers the same business activities as the C2 but allows multiple entries over a longer validity period, with each stay limited (typically 60 days per visit). It suits founders and buyers who travel to Indonesia several times a year. Available validity periods and fees are listed on the official D2 page.

4. E28A investor KITAS: residence for PT PMA shareholders

If you own shares in an Indonesian foreign-investment company (PT PMA, see our PT PMA guide), the E28A investor KITAS lets you live in Indonesia and serve on the board of directors or board of commissioners of the company you invest in.

Shareholding threshold: confirm before you plan your capital. Immigration sets its own minimum shareholding for the investor KITAS, separately from the company capital rules. Specialist advisers reported in 2025–2026 that the applicant must personally hold shares worth IDR 10 billion. Older guides (including an earlier version of this article) quoted IDR 1 billion. What the investor KITAS gives you

  • A stay permit (usually 1 or 2 years) with re-entry, renewable while you remain a qualifying shareholder.
  • The right to act as director or commissioner of your own PT PMA. Advisers report that investor KITAS holders are exempt from the foreign-worker levy (DKP-TKA) that applies to work KITAS holders.
  • The possibility to sponsor family members (see below).
  • A route towards a permanent stay permit (KITAP) after several years, subject to the rules in force at the time.

5. E33G remote worker visa

The E33G stay permit is for people who live in Indonesia but work for an employer or clients outside Indonesia. It is issued for one year. The published requirements include proof of income from abroad of at least USD 60,000 per year, an employment contract or proof of your foreign company, health insurance and sufficient funds. Income from Indonesian sources is not allowed on this visa.

Living in Indonesia for more than 183 days in a 12-month period can make you an Indonesian tax resident. Get tax advice from a qualified tax consultant before you move.

6. Family members

Spouses and children of a KITAS holder can apply for a dependant stay permit in the E31 family series, which follows the main permit holder's validity. The exact sub-code depends on the family relationship confirm sub-code with immigration. Marriage and birth certificates issued in Portugal usually need an apostille and a translation.

7. How the application works

  1. Check your purpose and documents: we go through your plans, passport and supporting documents with you and help you identify the right visa.
  2. Sponsor and online filing: your sponsor (for example your Indonesian business partner or your PT PMA) and you complete the application on the e-Visa portal.
  3. Approval: the e-Visa is sent by email. You do not need to visit the Indonesian Embassy in Lisbon for most e-Visas.
  4. After arrival (KITAS only): biometric registration at the local immigration office and activation of the stay permit.

Processing times vary by visa type and by immigration office. Allow extra time around Indonesian public holidays.

8. Common compliance pitfalls

  • Working on a visit visa. This can lead to deportation and an entry ban.
  • Overstaying. The overstay charge is IDR 1,000,000 per day.
  • Not reporting changes. KITAS holders must report changes of address, employer or civil status to immigration.
  • Mismatched sponsor. Your sponsor and the activity on your visa must match what you actually do in Indonesia.

9. Frequently asked questions

Does the B211A visa still exist?

No. Indonesia now labels its visas with index codes. For a single-entry business visit, the C2 business visit visa replaced the old B211A business route. You apply for it online at evisa.imigrasi.go.id.

Can Portuguese citizens get a visa on arrival in Indonesia?

Yes. Portuguese passport holders can use the visa on arrival (also available online as e-VoA) for a 30-day stay for tourism, business meetings, buying goods or transit. It does not allow work.

Can I work in Indonesia on a C2 or D2 business visa?

No. Business visit visas allow meetings, negotiations, market research and similar activities, but not employment or receiving payment from an Indonesian company. Working requires a work-based stay permit (KITAS).

What is the E28A investor KITAS?

E28A is the investor stay permit for foreigners who invest in an Indonesian company, usually a PT PMA. It allows them to sit on the board of directors or commissioners of that company. The minimum shareholding that immigration requires has changed several times, so confirm the current figure with the Directorate General of Immigration before you plan your capital.

1. Os vistos em resumo

A Indonésia deixou de usar designações como "B211A". Cada visto tem agora um código de índice e o pedido é feito online no portal e-Visa da Direção-Geral de Imigração.

  • Visto à chegada (e-VoA): 30 dias, prorrogável uma vez. Serve para turismo e reuniões de negócios.
  • C2, visto de visita de negócios: entrada única, 60 dias, prorrogável na Indonésia. Substitui o antigo B211A para fins de negócios.
  • D2: visto de negócios de entradas múltiplas para viagens frequentes.
  • E28A, KITAS de investidor: autorização de estadia para sócios de uma PT PMA.
  • E33G, trabalhador remoto: 1 ano, para quem trabalha para entidades fora da Indonésia (rendimento mínimo de 60 000 USD/ano).

Nenhum visto de visita permite trabalhar ou receber pagamentos de uma entidade indonésia.

2. KITAS de investidor (E28A)

Permite residir na Indonésia e integrar a administração ou o conselho de comissários da sua PT PMA. A imigração fixa um valor mínimo de participação próprio, distinto das regras de capital da empresa. Fontes especializadas referem 10 mil milhões IDR em ações detidas pelo requerente.
