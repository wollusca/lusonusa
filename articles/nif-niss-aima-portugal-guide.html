1. NIF: Número de Identificação Fiscal

The NIF is Portugal's 9-digit tax identification number. You need it to sign a lease, open a bank account, set up utilities and phone contracts, and for almost any official transaction.

How to get one

  • In Portugal: at a Tax Authority (Finanças) service desk with your passport and proof of address.
  • From Indonesia, before you move: through a representative in Portugal acting under a power of attorney.
Fiscal representative or electronic notifications? Non-residents from outside the EU/EEA have traditionally needed a representante fiscal. Since July 2022, those with no ongoing tax obligations in Portugal can instead sign up for the Tax Authority's electronic notifications on the Portal das Finanças. If you will have Portuguese income, property or a business activity, a fiscal representative is still required. confirm for your case

2. NISS: Número de Identificação da Segurança Social

The NISS is your 11-digit social security number. You need it to work as an employee, to register as self-employed (trabalhador independente), and for social security contributions and benefits.

  • Online form: foreign citizens can request a NISS on the Segurança Social portal ("Atribuição de NISS"), attaching a valid identity document.
  • Through your employer: an employer can request the NISS when hiring you.

Registration with the national health service (SNS, for a número de utente) is a separate step at your local health centre.

3. Opening a Portuguese bank account

Most banks ask for:

  1. A valid passport.
  2. Your Portuguese NIF.
  3. Proof of address (in Portugal, or in Indonesia for non-residents).
  4. Proof of income or occupation, such as an employment contract, payslips or a tax return.

Requirements differ from bank to bank, and some only open accounts for non-residents in person. Documents not in Portuguese or English may need a certified translation.

4. Residence permit with AIMA

AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo) took over residence permits from the former SEF in October 2023.

No more "manifestação de interesse". Decree-Law 37-A/2024 ended new expressions of interest from 4 June 2024. Indonesian citizens now generally need the right residence visa first, issued by the Portuguese Embassy in Jakarta, and then apply for the residence permit with AIMA in Portugal.

Common residence visa types

  • Work: employment with a Portuguese employer, or a job-seeker visa.
  • D2: entrepreneurs and independent professionals.
  • D4: study.
  • D7: passive income (pensions, rental or investment income).
  • D8: remote work for employers or clients outside Portugal.

Preparing for your AIMA appointment

Bring a complete file: passport and visa, proof of accommodation (a registered lease, or an atestado de residência from your Junta de Freguesia), proof of means, health insurance where required, and a criminal record certificate (SKCK) with an apostille and a certified Portuguese translation. AIMA may ask for originals, so bring them.

6. Frequently asked questions

Can I get a Portuguese NIF from Indonesia before I move?

Yes. You can request a NIF from abroad through a representative in Portugal. Non-residents from outside the EU/EEA usually appoint a fiscal representative, or may instead sign up for the Tax Authority's electronic notifications if they have no ongoing tax obligations in Portugal. Ask the Tax Authority (Finanças) which option applies to you.

Can I still apply for residence in Portugal through a "manifestação de interesse"?

No. Decree-Law 37-A/2024 ended new expressions of interest from 4 June 2024. Indonesian citizens now generally need the appropriate Portuguese residence visa (for example a work, study, D7 or D8 visa) from the Portuguese Embassy in Jakarta before applying for a residence permit with AIMA in Portugal.

How do foreigners get a NISS in Portugal?

Foreign citizens can request a NISS through the online form on the Segurança Social portal, attaching a valid identity document. An employer can also request it when hiring you.

1. NIF (nomor pajak Portugal)

NIF adalah nomor identitas pajak 9 digit. NIF diperlukan untuk menyewa tempat tinggal, membuka rekening bank, dan hampir semua urusan resmi. NIF bisa diurus di kantor Finanças di Portugal, atau dari Indonesia melalui perwakilan dengan surat kuasa.

WNI yang belum tinggal di Portugal biasanya menunjuk perwakilan fiskal (representante fiscal). Sejak Juli 2022, yang tidak memiliki kewajiban pajak di Portugal dapat memilih notifikasi elektronik di Portal das Finanças sebagai gantinya. konfirmasi sesuai kasus Anda

2. NISS (nomor jaminan sosial)

NISS adalah nomor jaminan sosial 11 digit untuk bekerja, mendaftar sebagai pekerja mandiri, dan membayar iuran. Permohonan bisa dilakukan lewat formulir online di portal Segurança Social atau melalui pemberi kerja.

3. Izin tinggal melalui AIMA

AIMA menggantikan SEF sejak Oktober 2023. Jalur manifestação de interesse sudah ditutup sejak 4 Juni 2024 (Decreto-Lei 37-A/2024). Sekarang WNI umumnya harus memiliki visa tinggal yang sesuai (kerja, studi, D2, D7, atau D8) dari Kedutaan Besar Portugal di Jakarta, lalu mengajukan izin tinggal ke AIMA setelah tiba di Portugal.

Siapkan berkas lengkap: paspor dan visa, bukti tempat tinggal, bukti dana, asuransi kesehatan bila diminta, serta SKCK yang sudah diapostille dan diterjemahkan ke bahasa Portugis dengan terjemahan tersertifikasi.
