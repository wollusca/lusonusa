1. NIF: Número de Identificação Fiscal

The NIF is Portugal's 9-digit tax identification number (Decree-Law 14/2013). You need it to sign a lease, open a bank account, set up utilities and phone contracts, and for almost any official transaction.

How to get one

  • In Portugal: at a Tax Authority (Finanças) service desk. Bring your passport (with your entry visa) and proof of address (gov.pt).
  • From Indonesia, before you move: through a representative in Portugal acting under a power of attorney. Since July 2025 the representative can also file the request online through e-balcão on the Portal das Finanças. A power of attorney signed in Indonesia needs an apostille, and a translation if it is not in Portuguese.
Do you need a fiscal representative?
  • At first issue: no. The Tax Authority's leaflet on NIFs for foreign non-residents says that appointing a fiscal representative is not mandatory when a NIF is first assigned.
  • Later dealings (for example property or income in Portugal): since Decree-Law 44/2022 (July 2022), a non-resident from outside the EU/EEA can avoid appointing a representative by signing up for the Portal das Finanças electronic notifications (General Tax Law art. 19(15)). If you later cancel that sign-up, the cancellation only takes effect once you appoint a representative (art. 19(16)).
  • VAT-registered business activity: still needs a VAT representative secondary source only.

The access code for the Portal das Finanças is posted to your address, so in practice you sign up after the NIF is issued. The gov.pt migrants page and some tax offices still say that non-residents must appoint a representative, so confirm with Finanças which option applies to you.

2. NISS: Número de Identificação da Segurança Social

The NISS is your 11-digit social security number (Law 110/2009, Contributory Code). You need it to work as an employee, to register as self-employed (trabalhador independente), and for social security contributions and benefits.

  • Online form: foreign citizens can request a NISS on the Segurança Social portal ("Atribuição de NISS"), attaching a passport, work visa, residence title or residence authorisation. After approval you receive a registered letter, and you must collect the NISS in person at a Segurança Social office (ISS practical guide).
  • Through your employer: an employer can request the NISS when hiring you, and must then report the employment separately in Segurança Social Direta.

Registration with the national health service (SNS, for a número de utente) is a separate step at your local health centre. If you have a Portuguese address, gov.pt offers a joint request for NIF, NISS and SNS number.

3. Opening a Portuguese bank account

Banks must identify their customers under anti-money-laundering rules (Law 83/2017), so most ask for:

  1. A valid passport.
  2. Your Portuguese NIF.
  3. Proof of address (in Portugal, or in Indonesia for non-residents).
  4. Proof of income or occupation, such as an employment contract, payslips or a tax return.

Requirements differ from bank to bank, and some only open accounts for non-residents in person. Documents not in Portuguese or English may need a certified translation.

4. Residence permit with AIMA

AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo) was created by Decree-Law 41/2023 and took over residence permits from the former SEF on 29 October 2023.

No more "manifestação de interesse". Decree-Law 37-A/2024 ended new expressions of interest from 4 June 2024, and Law 61/2025 confirmed this. Indonesian citizens now need the right residence visa first (Foreigners Act, Law 23/2007, arts. 58 and 77), then apply for the residence permit with AIMA in Portugal.

Where to apply for the visa

In Indonesia, Portuguese visa applications are lodged through VFS Global (centres in Jakarta and Bali), and the Portuguese Embassy in Jakarta decides. See the Portuguese visa portal.

Common residence visa types

  • Work: employment with a Portuguese employer. Since 23 October 2025 (Law 61/2025), the general job-seeker visa no longer exists; only a job-seeker visa for highly qualified workers remains check implementing portaria.
  • D2: entrepreneurs and independent professionals (Law 23/2007 art. 60).
  • D4: study (art. 62).
  • D7: passive income such as pensions, rental or investment income (art. 58, with Regulatory Decree 84/2007).
  • D8: remote work for employers or clients outside Portugal (art. 61-B).

Booking your AIMA appointment

If you entered with a consular residence visa and are not a CPLP national, AIMA asks you to request the residence-permit appointment through its contact form ("Autorização de Residência com Visto Consular (Não CPLP)"), giving your passport and visa numbers. AIMA says an online platform for these cases is being introduced.

Preparing for your AIMA appointment

Bring a complete file (Law 23/2007 art. 77(1)): passport and visa, proof of accommodation (a registered lease, or an atestado de residência from your Junta de Freguesia), proof of means, health insurance where required, and a criminal record certificate (SKCK) with an apostille and a certified Portuguese translation. Under Indonesian Police Regulation (Perpol) 1/2026, each SKCK covers one purpose and one destination country and is valid for 6 months, so request it "for residence in Portugal" close to your appointment. AIMA may ask for originals, so bring them.

6. Frequently asked questions

Can I get a Portuguese NIF from Indonesia before I move?

Yes. A representative in Portugal holding your power of attorney can request it at a tax office or, since July 2025, online through e-balcão. According to the Tax Authority, appointing a fiscal representative is not mandatory when a NIF is first assigned to a non-resident. Later, a non-EU/EEA non-resident can avoid needing one by signing up for electronic notifications (General Tax Law art. 19(15)). Confirm with Finanças, as some offices still ask for a representative.

Can I still apply for residence in Portugal through a "manifestação de interesse"?

No. Decree-Law 37-A/2024 ended new expressions of interest from 4 June 2024. Law 61/2025 confirmed this. Indonesian citizens now need the appropriate Portuguese residence visa (for example a work, study, D7 or D8 visa), applied for through VFS Global in Jakarta or Bali and decided by the Portuguese Embassy, before requesting a residence permit appointment with AIMA in Portugal.

How do foreigners get a NISS in Portugal?

Foreign citizens can request a NISS through the online form on the Segurança Social portal, attaching a passport, visa or residence document. After approval, the NISS is collected in person at a Segurança Social office. An employer can also request it when hiring you.

1. NIF (nomor pajak Portugal)

NIF adalah nomor identitas pajak 9 digit. NIF diperlukan untuk menyewa tempat tinggal, membuka rekening bank, dan hampir semua urusan resmi. NIF bisa diurus di kantor Finanças di Portugal, atau dari Indonesia melalui perwakilan dengan surat kuasa.

Menurut brosur Otoritas Pajak, penunjukan perwakilan fiskal (representante fiscal) tidak wajib saat NIF pertama kali diterbitkan untuk WNA non-residen. Sejak Decreto-Lei 44/2022, non-residen dari luar UE/EEA yang kemudian punya urusan pajak (misalnya properti atau penghasilan) bisa tidak menunjuk perwakilan dengan mendaftar notifikasi elektronik di Portal das Finanças (Lei Geral Tributária pasal 19(15)). Kegiatan usaha yang terdaftar PPN tetap memerlukan perwakilan PPN. Sejak Juli 2025, permohonan NIF juga bisa diajukan online lewat e-balcão oleh kuasa Anda. Beberapa kantor masih meminta perwakilan, jadi konfirmasikan ke Finanças.

2. NISS (nomor jaminan sosial)

NISS adalah nomor jaminan sosial 11 digit untuk bekerja, mendaftar sebagai pekerja mandiri, dan membayar iuran. Permohonan bisa dilakukan lewat formulir online di portal Segurança Social atau melalui pemberi kerja. Setelah disetujui, NISS harus diambil langsung di kantor Segurança Social.

3. Izin tinggal melalui AIMA

AIMA menggantikan SEF sejak Oktober 2023. Jalur manifestação de interesse sudah ditutup sejak 4 Juni 2024 (Decreto-Lei 37-A/2024, ditegaskan oleh Lei 61/2025). Sekarang WNI harus memiliki visa tinggal yang sesuai (kerja, studi, D2, D7, atau D8). Permohonan visa diajukan melalui VFS Global (Jakarta dan Bali) dan diputuskan oleh Kedutaan Besar Portugal. Setelah tiba di Portugal, pemegang visa konsuler (non-CPLP) meminta jadwal izin tinggal melalui formulir kontak AIMA. Sejak 23 Oktober 2025 (Lei 61/2025), visa pencari kerja umum dihapus; yang tersisa hanya visa pencari kerja berkualifikasi tinggi.

Siapkan berkas lengkap: paspor dan visa, bukti tempat tinggal, bukti dana, asuransi kesehatan bila diminta, serta SKCK yang sudah diapostille dan diterjemahkan ke bahasa Portugis dengan terjemahan tersertifikasi. Menurut Perpol 1/2026, setiap SKCK hanya untuk satu keperluan dan satu negara tujuan, dan berlaku 6 bulan; ajukan "untuk tinggal di Portugal" menjelang jadwal AIMA Anda.
