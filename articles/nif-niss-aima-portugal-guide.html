1. Getting a NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal)

The NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal) is the fundamental 9-digit Portuguese tax identification number. It is required for signing lease contracts, opening a bank account, setting up utilities, and executing any transaction in Portugal.

Non-EU citizens (including Indonesian passport holders) who are not yet resident in Portugal require a official Fiscal Representative (Representante Fiscal) registered with the Portuguese Tax Authority (Autoridade Tributária e Aduaneira).

2. Obtaining a NISS (Número de Identificação da Segurança Social)

The NISS is Portugal's 11-digit Social Security Number. It is legally mandated for employment contracts, freelance activity (Trabalhador Independente), and accessing the National Health Service (SNS):

  • For Employed Staff: Applied by your local employer upon signing a contract.
  • For Self-Employed & Entrepreneurs: Filed online directly through the Segurança Social Direta portal with an active NIF.

3. Opening a Portuguese Bank Account

To pay rent and satisfy AIMA residence proof of funds, opening a local SEPA bank account (such as Millennium BCP, Caixa Geral de Depósitos, or Banco CTT) requires:

  1. Valid passport & Indonesian National ID (KTP).
  2. Active Portuguese NIF document.
  3. Proof of Address (Indonesian utility bill or sworn translation).
  4. Proof of Income / Occupation (employment contract or tax return).

4. AIMA Residence Registration & Appointments

Replacing the former SEF, the AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo) oversees all residence permits (D2 Entrepreneur, D7 Passive Income, Student, and Work Residence permits).

Preparing your full document portfolio—including sworn translations of civil certificates, proof of accommodation (Atestado de Residência), and clean police clearance—is vital prior to your scheduled AIMA biometric appointment.

1. Pengurusan NIF (Nomor Pajak Portugal)

NIF (Número de Identificação Fiscal) adalah nomor identitas pajak 9 digit wajib di Portugal. NIF diperlukan untuk menyewa apartemen, membuka rekening bank, dan mengurus izin tinggal.

WNI yang belum bertempat tinggal resmi di Portugal wajib memiliki Perwakilan Fiskal (Representante Fiscal) terdaftar di kantor pajak Portugal.

2. Pengurusan NISS (Jaminan Sosial)

NISS adalah nomor jaminan sosial 11 digit yang diperlukan untuk bekerja atau mendaftar jaminan kesehatan di Portugal.

3. Pendaftaran Izin Tinggal AIMA

AIMA (Agência para a Integração, Migrações e Asilo) mengurus semua izin tinggal resmi (Izin Tinggal D2, D7, Pelajar, dan Kerja) untuk WNI di Portugal.
