2. Data we handle

When you browse this website

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers, and you do not need an account. Our hosting provider and the third-party services listed in section 6 automatically receive the technical data needed to deliver the page: your IP address, browser, device and operating system, the page requested, the referring page, and the date and time.

When you contact us or use our services

When you message us on WhatsApp or by email, or when we work with you, we handle:

  • Contact data: your name, phone number, email address and, on WhatsApp, your profile name and picture.
  • What you tell us: the content of your messages, such as the procedure you need help with, your appointments and your general situation.
  • Business information (for companies): company name, contact person, the market or suppliers you are interested in.
  • Billing data: invoices, payment confirmations and the name of the paying person or company.

3. Your documents

Our services are information, orientation and in-person accompaniment. We do not need, and do not intend to collect or store, digital copies of identity or immigration documents such as passports, residence permits, visas or AIMA files.

  • In person: when we accompany you to an appointment (for example at AIMA, Finanças, Segurança Social or a bank), we may see your documents. They stay with you; we do not copy or keep them.
  • If you send documents anyway: sometimes clients send a photo or file, for example to ask us whether a document is the right one. Please only do this if it is really necessary, and cover any information we do not need. We use a document you send us only to answer that specific question. We do not share it with anyone and do not use it for anything else, and we delete it, including from our phones and chats, [within X days after the matter is closed].
  • Authorities and professionals: where a procedure requires documents, you submit them yourself to the authority, or to the licensed professional you choose, such as a lawyer, notary or accountant.
  • Translations: if you order a translation, the document has to be read to be translated. The translator who delivers it receives the document as the controller for that service, uses it only to produce and certify the translation, and tells you how long they keep it.

Some documents can reveal sensitive information, such as health or criminal-record data. Please do not send us this kind of document at all.

5. Who we share data with

We do not share your documents. We share other personal data only where you ask us to or the law requires it:

  • Within the network: when you ask for a service, your contact details and a description of what you need are passed to the professional who will deliver it, so they can prepare a quote.
  • To book appointments for you: the minimum details an authority, bank or office requires to make the booking (usually your name and contact details), and only with your agreement.
  • Professionals you choose: if you ask us to introduce you to a lawyer, notary, accountant or other professional, we pass on your contact details and a short description of your matter. They work independently under their own terms and privacy policies.
  • Service providers (processors) who help us run our business, listed in section 6. They may only use the data on our instructions.
  • Courts, regulators or law enforcement when we are legally required to disclose information.

7. International transfers

We work between Portugal and Indonesia, so your data may be handled in both countries. Some professionals in the network are based in Indonesia, which does not currently have an adequacy decision from the European Commission. Where personal data from the EEA is handled in Indonesia, this is necessary to provide the service you asked for (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR), or we use the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses where they apply.

Some of our service providers (GitHub, Google, Meta) are based in or transfer data to the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. How long we keep data

  • Documents you sent us unprompted: deleted [within X days after the matter is closed], or sooner if you ask.
  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: up to 12 months after our last message, then deleted.
  • Client correspondence: for the duration of the engagement and up to 12 months afterwards, unless needed for a claim.

9. Security

We limit access to client information to the professionals working on your matter, use encrypted channels (HTTPS, end-to-end encrypted WhatsApp) where available, and protect our accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication. No system is completely secure, which is another reason we ask you not to send documents we do not need. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

10. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the GDPR gives you the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data erased, including any document you sent us;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already took place.

Send requests to contact@lusonusa.agency. We will reply within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa, www.cnpd.pt. You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

11. Indonesian data subjects (UU PDP)

For clients in Indonesia, we handle personal data in line with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi). You have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent and objection, among others. Contact us at the address above to exercise them.

12. Children

Our website and services are not aimed at children. Where a matter involves a minor, we deal only with the child’s parent or legal guardian.
