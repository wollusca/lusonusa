2. Data we collect

When you browse this website

We do not run analytics or advertising trackers, and we do not ask you to create an account. Our hosting provider and the third-party services listed in section 5 automatically receive technical data needed to deliver the page: your IP address, browser type, device and operating system, the page requested, the referring page and the date and time.

When you contact us

When you message us on WhatsApp or by email we receive your name, phone number or email address, profile name and picture (WhatsApp), the content of your messages and any files you attach.

When you use our services

Depending on the service you ask for (for example visa or residence applications, NIF/NISS registration, company setup, bank account opening or certified translation), we may process:

  • Identity data: full name, date and place of birth, nationality, passport or national ID (KTP) details and copies, photographs.
  • Contact and address data: addresses in Portugal and Indonesia, phone numbers, email.
  • Immigration and civil-status data: visa and residence permit history, AIMA or Imigrasi case references, birth, marriage or family documents, and criminal-record certificates where an authority requires them.
  • Tax, social-security and financial data: NIF, NISS, NPWP, bank statements, proof of income or means of subsistence, company and shareholder information.
  • Documents for translation: whatever the document you send for translation contains.
  • Billing data: invoices, payment confirmations and the name of the paying person or company.

Some of these documents may reveal sensitive information (for example health insurance details or criminal-record certificates). We only ask for what the relevant authority, notary or partner requires for the service you requested, and we process it only to carry out that service.

6. International transfers

We work between Portugal and Indonesia, so your data will be handled in both countries. Our team and controller are based in Indonesia, which does not currently have an adequacy decision from the European Commission. Where we transfer personal data from the EEA to Indonesia, we do so because the transfer is necessary to perform the contract you asked us to carry out (Art. 49(1)(b) GDPR), or under the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses where they apply.

Some of our service providers (GitHub, Google, Meta) are based in or transfer data to the United States. These transfers rely on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified, or on Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement: up to 12 months after our last message, then deleted.
  • Client files and correspondence: for the duration of the engagement and then for up to 10 years, to meet accounting and tax obligations and to handle possible claims.
  • Copies of identity and immigration documents: deleted or returned within 6 months of the case closing, unless you ask us to keep them for a follow-up application or the law requires longer retention.
  • Website server logs: kept by GitHub according to its own retention policy.

8. Security

We limit access to client documents to the team members and partners working on your case, use encrypted channels (HTTPS, end-to-end encrypted WhatsApp) where available, and use accounts protected with strong passwords and two-factor authentication. No system is completely secure, so please avoid sending documents we have not asked for. If a breach is likely to put your rights at risk, we will notify you and the competent authority as the law requires.

9. Your rights

If you are in the EEA, the GDPR gives you the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have data erased, where there is no longer a reason to keep it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing, including processing based on legitimate interest;
  • receive data you gave us in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting processing that already took place.

Send requests to contact@lusonusa.agency. We will reply within one month and may ask you to confirm your identity first.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD), Av. D. Carlos I, 134, 1.º, 1200-651 Lisboa, www.cnpd.pt. You can also complain to the authority in the EU country where you live or work.

10. Indonesian data subjects (UU PDP)

For clients in Indonesia, we process personal data in line with Law No. 27 of 2022 on Personal Data Protection (Undang-Undang Nomor 27 Tahun 2022 tentang Pelindungan Data Pribadi). You have rights to information, access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent and objection, among others. Contact us at the address above to exercise them.

11. Children

Our website and services are not aimed at children. Where a service involves a minor (for example a family reunification application), we only process the child’s data on the instructions of a parent or legal guardian.
