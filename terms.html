10. Right of withdrawal (consumers)

If you are a consumer (an individual acting outside your trade, business or profession), you may withdraw from a service contract within 14 days of concluding it, without giving any reason and without cost, under articles 10 to 17 of Decreto-Lei n.º 24/2014. This applies to contracts concluded at a distance (WhatsApp, email, phone) and to contracts concluded face to face away from business premises, for example at a meeting or during an accompaniment.

  • How to withdraw: send the professional named on your quote a clear statement (for example by email to contact@lusonusa.agency or by WhatsApp to +351 930 534 440, which will be passed on), or use the model form below. It is enough to send it before the 14 days end.
  • Refund: all payments received are refunded within 14 days of the day we learn of your decision, using the same means of payment unless you agree otherwise.
  • Starting within the 14 days: if you want the service to start before the 14 days end, you must ask for this expressly in writing. If you then withdraw, you pay an amount proportional to the service actually provided until you told us, calculated on the total agreed price (art. 15.º). If the service is fully performed within the 14 days at your express request, and you acknowledged this, you lose the right of withdrawal (art. 17.º, n.º 1, al. a)).
  • Business clients: the right of withdrawal does not apply to contracts with companies or professionals acting for the purposes of their business.
